Salapify

A budget and debt tracker for the Philippines, where debt means both directions: what you owe, and what is owed to you.

  • Offline. It works with no signal and no load.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No ads, and no trackers.
  • Your data stays on your phone, encrypted, and leaves only in a backup you make yourself.

Privacy policy Source on GitHub

Salapify is a personal finance tracker. It does not lend money, offer credit, or handle investments. It is not yet on Google Play.

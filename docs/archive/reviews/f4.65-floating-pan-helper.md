# f4.65 Floating Pan helper, implementation report

Stamp: f4.65 (ships over the air, no version bump)
Branch: claude/salapify-accounts-tab-overhaul-vm3gm6
Feature source: v4.58 spec, feature 4 of 4 the founder chose to build.

## Scope

Pan's floating helper: a small draggable Pan that rides the edge of the four
main tabs and, on a tap, opens a sheet of swipeable tips. Each tip sends the
person to the right screen, and the last opens Pan's existing plain-words Q&A.

## What it reuses (so it cannot drift)

- The face is the one shared `PanMascot` (a fixed orange on every theme).
- The tips are pure data in `money/pan_tips.dart`.
- The navigation is handed back to the shell through a single `onTipTap`
  callback; the bubble never learns the route map. The shell's exhaustive
  `switch` (analyzer-enforced) maps every tip target to a real destination, and
  Pan's own Q&A screen and route registry are unchanged.

## Honesty

The tips are signposts, not claims about the person's own money. Each describes a
shipped feature (Safe to spend on Accounts, the Credit Radar and Avalanche vs
Snowball on Debts, spending on Insights), and the last is the open door to Pan's
Q&A, whose copy states it reads only what is on the phone and never makes up a
number. A content test asserts the shape and forbids em or en dashes.

## Behaviour

Chat-head shaped: drag anywhere and on release it snaps to the nearer side and
persists the side and vertical fraction, so it is never in the way and never
lost. It defaults to the LEFT edge so it never stacks on the Log FAB at
bottom-right. It is only mounted on the resident bar tabs (a pushed screen or a
focused task covers it), and it can be turned off in Appearance (Show Pan
helper, on by default).

## What changed

- `flutter/lib/money/pan_tips.dart` (new pure tips data).
- `flutter/lib/widgets/pan_helper_bubble.dart` (new draggable bubble and
  swipeable tip sheet, reuses `PanMascot` and the Barako/AppText system).
- `flutter/lib/screens/shell.dart` (mounts the bubble over the bar-tab body in a
  Stack when enabled, adds a Pan opener and the tip-to-navigation mapping).
- `flutter/lib/screens/appearance.dart` (a Show Pan helper toggle).
- `flutter/lib/main.dart` (stamp f4.65).
- Tests: `pan_tips_test.dart`, `pan_helper_bubble_test.dart`,
  `pan_helper_shot.dart`.

## What did NOT change

No money math, no stored money or account data, no schema, no migration. Two new
NON-money view settings (`panHelperEnabled`, and the remembered `panHelperSide`
and `panHelperY`). Pan is drawn from the existing bundled PNGs, no new asset, so
it ships over the air.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues on the whole tree, pinned 3.44.6.
- pan_tips_test.dart: content and dash guards.
- pan_helper_bubble_test.dart: opens the sheet, reports the tapped tip, swipes to
  the Ask Pan door, and the drag snaps and persists a side. A real widget-test
  gotcha was worked through rather than papered over: the store persists through
  a serialized async write that needs real async in a widget test (flushed with
  `tester.runAsync`), and the synthetic pan gesture needed a slop-exceeding first
  move with pumps between so the pan recognizer wins the arena.
- Full local suite green.
- Rendered dark and looked at, on the real shell and as the tip sheet; both sent
  to the founder.

## Deferred (none blocking)

1. An animated Rive Pan when a rigged file exists (the swap point is already
   documented in `pan_mascot.dart`).
2. Tips that react to the person's own state (a nudge when a card is over the
   30 percent line), which would move the helper from honest signposts toward
   the coach's territory and should be designed with the financial-coach.
3. A first-run coach-mark pointing at Pan so a new user learns he is draggable.

## Risks

Low. The helper reads no money and computes nothing; it navigates to screens
that carry the real figures. The only care taken is that it never covers a
focused task (mounted on bar tabs only), never stacks on the Log FAB (defaults
left), and can be turned off.

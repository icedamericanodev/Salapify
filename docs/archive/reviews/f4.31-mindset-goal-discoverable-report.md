# f4.31 Goal-impact always discoverable on Impact, report

Founder feedback: on a fresh phone they could not see the goal-impact card. Root
cause: f4.30 rendered the card only when a savings goal already existed, and a
new install has none, so the whole section was invisible and undiscoverable.

## What changed

- flutter/lib/screens/mindset_flow.dart: the goal section now ALWAYS shows on
  Step 2. With at least one open goal it renders the full before/after card
  (f4.30); with none it renders a prompt ("Set a savings goal and this will show
  how much a buy sets it back, and about how much later it would land").
- lib/main.dart: stamp f4.31.

## Also traced (reported, not changed)

Two entry points to Money Mindset exist: the menu opens the new flow
(MindsetTodayScreen -> Log a Decision -> the 4-step flow) where the score gauge,
the "How we score this" explainer, and the goal impact all live; learn.dart's
"mindset" route still opens the OLDER single-screen MindsetScreen. A lesson link
therefore lands on a different, older experience. That is a navigation/UX
decision for the founder and was not changed silently.

Where to find each on the phone: Money mindset -> Log a Decision -> enter an
amount (Step 1) -> Step 2 shows the score gauge, the explainer link, and the
goal section.

## Money and data

PRESENTATION ONLY. No score change, no money math, no storage, no schema. Same
score-blind goal treatment as f4.30.

# f4.33 Remove the retired old Money Mindset screen, report

Founder approved the deliberate removal proposed in f4.32. After f4.32 re-pointed
the last links to the new flow, the old single-screen MindsetScreen was
unreachable in the app; this deletes it and everything that only existed for it.

## What was removed

- flutter/lib/screens/mindset.dart (3,521 lines), the old single screen.
- Its four screen-only test files: mindset_screen_test.dart,
  mindset_purchase_screen_test.dart, mindset_waiting_screen_test.dart,
  mindset_shot.dart.
- The three old-screen render blocks and the import in test/screens_shot.dart.
- Its entries in the accounting tests: the import, the pump-map entry, and the
  swept-file list entry in screen_readability_test.dart; the map entry in
  type_discipline_test.dart; and the shot_reachability exemption (now back to an
  empty map, the guard's intended state).
- The orphaned golden PNGs the removed shots produced (only PNGs no longer
  referenced by any test).

## Why it is safe

- No lib code imported mindset.dart after f4.32 (its only two links, the menu and
  the lesson reader, moved to the new flow in f4.32).
- The underlying money math keeps its unit coverage: mindset_purchase_test,
  mindset_decision_test, goal_plan_test, mindset_decisions_test all remain. Only
  UI-wiring coverage of a screen that no longer ships was lost.
- The new dashboard and flow (and their shots) are the sole Money Mindset
  experience, already covered by their own tests and shots.

## Money and data

None. Deletion of dead, unreachable code: no money engine, storage, schema, or
shipping screen changed.

## Validation

- flutter analyze (whole project): 0 issues.
- Full suite green; test count dropped from 3020 to 2931 as the four screen-only
  test files went with the screen. No dangling references.

## Risks

Low. Pure removal of unreachable code, guarded by a clean whole-project analyze
and the full suite. Ships over the air.

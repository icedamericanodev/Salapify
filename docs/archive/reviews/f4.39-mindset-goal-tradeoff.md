# f4.39 Money Mindset "What this costs your goal" improvements

Stamp: f4.39
Branch: claude/execute-this-4is90d
Type: presentation only, ships over the air (Shorebird patch)

## Scope

The founder asked to keep and improve/"explore more" the "What this costs your
goal" section of the Impact step. Two experts explored first: a financial-coach on
the money framing and a flutter-ux-craftsman on the layout. Their one disagreement
(whether to surface the peso opportunity cost) was resolved by weaving it into the
footer sentence rather than adding a competing big number.

## What changed

Presentation only. No money math, no data, no schema, no write path.

1. The delay is now a scannable chip beside the goal name (reusing the f4.38
   `_statusPill`; warningStrong for an emergency fund, else warning). It shows
   ONLY when the engine has a real deadline and pace, never a fabricated date.
2. The footer leads with the plain peso the buy would otherwise add to the goal,
   then an impact-scaled share clause:
   - small buy (under 10%): "a small slice of what you still need" (honest relief,
     deliberately NOT a green light),
   - 10% and up: "about N% of what you still need",
   - 100% and up: "more than the PHPX you still need for <goal>".
3. The emergency-fund "Careful" lead is earned: it fires only on a significant buy
   (25% or a real delay), and reads the quieter "This is your emergency fund."
   otherwise. The shield icon is the persistent marker.
4. When there is no delay to project (no deadline or no pace), the old unquantified
   "It slows it down" is replaced with an actionable "Set a target date in Goals to
   see how much later this lands."
5. Empty state refreshed (waiting icon, tighter copy). `_goalBar` caption gains
   maxLines + ellipsis so a 7-digit figure never overflows.

## What did NOT change

- No money value, sign, currency, precision, rounding, account relationship,
  transaction classification, calculation, reconciliation, schema, or stored data.
  Every figure (peso opportunity cost, percentOfRemaining, remaining, delay
  periods) is an existing tested goalTradeoff output. The After-bar denominator
  saved/(target+amt) is unchanged. No new projection or arithmetic was added.
- The two before/after bars are kept.
- Read-only money; no write path touched.
- Deferred, founder-gated: a standalone "save this instead" block or a numeric
  percentage-delta between the bars (new UI arithmetic, flagged by both experts);
  remembering the last-chosen goal (needs a stored key). The impact thresholds
  (small under 10, significant 25, over 100) are product-tunable.

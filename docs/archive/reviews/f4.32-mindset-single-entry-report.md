## Why an exemption, and the follow-up

The old MindsetScreen is now built nowhere in lib (its only two links were these
two), so the shot-reachability guard flags its three shots in screens_shot.dart.
The screen itself is retained because mindset_waiting_screen_test still mounts it
for the waiting-list flow. Rather than half-remove it, this phase exempts it with
a specific reason.

FOLLOW-UP (founder decision, a deliberate deletion): remove mindset.dart, its
three shots in screens_shot.dart, the orphaned golden PNGs, and
mindset_waiting_screen_test together, then delete the exemption. Deleting a whole
shipped screen is a material change, so it is proposed rather than done silently.
Coverage is not lost meanwhile: the new dashboard and flow shots already cover
the Waiting section, the wins, the 30-day snapshot, and the purchase-type paths.

# f4.57 Accounts tab overhaul: floating 3D card, skins, action sheet, statement

Stamp: f4.57. Branch: claude/salapify-accounts-tab-overhaul-vm3gm6.
Ships over the air (Dart only, no new native dependency, no asset change).

## Scope

A presentation and tooling upgrade to the Accounts tab, built strictly as an
enhancement of what already exists. Four pieces, all offline and honest:

1. A steady, font independent masked card number (`CardNumberMask`) that fixes
   the dark mode number distortion and jitter by drawing the mask as fixed
   geometric dots and the last four as tabular figures.
2. An interactive 3D floating card (`FloatingPanCard`) that tilts on drag, shows
   a soft specular highlight, and unmasks the last four on tap with a haptic.
3. A per account action sheet reachable by long pressing a credit card, wiring
   the card to flows the app already has: the ledger, logging money, transfer,
   edit, the account's own saved receiving QR, a PDF statement, and hide.
4. A Card Skin Studio: four finishes (Obsidian, Emerald, Gold, Platinum) chosen
   per account and stored locally.

## What changed

- NEW `flutter/lib/widgets/pan_mask_widget.dart`: `CardNumberMask`. Takes a
  nullable `last4` only; there is no way to pass a full number. Wired into
  `bank_card.dart` and `flip_bank_card.dart` in place of the old bullet string.
- NEW `flutter/lib/widgets/floating_pan_card.dart`: `FloatingPanCard`, composing
  the existing `BankCard` face (so it inherits the AA contrast guarantee) with a
  perspective tilt, a specular overlay, and tap to unmask.
- NEW `flutter/lib/widgets/account_action_sheet.dart`: `showAccountActionSheet`,
  a three tier sheet whose every action is a caller supplied callback onto an
  existing flow.
- NEW `flutter/lib/widgets/card_skin_studio.dart`: `showCardSkinStudio`, a picker
  with a live preview of the caller's own card, re seeded.
- NEW `flutter/lib/services/card_skins.dart`: the skin registry and
  `CardSkinStore`, a SharedPreferences backed, per account preference. NOT part
  of the backup.
- CHANGED `flutter/lib/data/export_files.dart`: added `accountStatementPdf`,
  `shareAccountStatementPdf`, `saveAccountStatementPdfToDevice`, and factored the
  shared `_pdfTheme()` font loader out of `reportPdf` (no behaviour change to the
  existing report).
- CHANGED `flutter/lib/screens/accounts.dart`: `_creditCardTile` now resolves the
  card's skin seed (repainting via a `ListenableBuilder` on `CardSkinStore`) and
  gains an `onLongPress` that opens the action sheet. New helpers
  `_floatingCardFor` and `_openAccountActions`. A plain tap still opens the full
  account details, unchanged.
- CHANGED `flutter/lib/main.dart`: loads `CardSkinStore.instance` at startup
  (cosmetic, error swallowing, never blocks startup).
- STAMP bumped to f4.57 in `lib/main.dart`.

## What did NOT change (the load bearing claim)

No money behaviour and no stored data. No value, sign, currency, precision,
rounding, transaction classification, account relationship, schema version,
migration, or backup format is touched:

- The masked number still shows only the last four, which is the only card digit
  data the app ever stores. `CardNumberMask` cannot render more than four digits
  by construction, and the card faces still pass the four only behind their
  existing reveal state.
- The statement PDF is read only. Its figures come from the golden locked
  `accountMonthFlow` (the same in/out/net the account detail screen shows) and
  `amountOf`; it invents no arithmetic and writes nothing.
- Card skins live only in SharedPreferences (`card_skins_v1`), keyed by account
  id. They never enter the Salapify backup blob or `sanitizeData`, so the backup
  goldens and the money engine are untouched. The honest cost: a skin does not
  travel when a backup is restored to a new phone, the same as the app lock latch
  and live FX rates.
- The archive toggle in the action sheet uses the existing `patchDebtMeta` with
  the already patchable `isArchived` key; it cannot move money or reclassify.

## Honesty decisions (founder confirmed)

- QR Ph shows the account's OWN saved receiving QR through the existing QR vault
  sheet; with none saved it routes to the account details to attach one. No
  fabricated QR Ph payload, and no `qr_flutter` dependency was added.
- No "freeze" control. The real, existing Archive/Hide toggle is used instead of
  a label that would freeze nothing.

## Deviation from the requested file paths

The request named `lib/features/accounts/...` paths. This repo has no
`lib/features/` tree; it uses `lib/screens` and `lib/widgets`. To avoid forking
the architecture, the new files live under `lib/widgets` and `lib/services`, and
the statement PDF was added to the existing `lib/data/export_files.dart` rather
than a new file, reusing its font loader and share/guard helpers.

## Independent QA (qa-tester agent)

An adversarial QA pass reviewed the whole diff. It confirmed the core contract
holds (only the last four are ever rendered, no money/schema/backup change, the
statement PDF resolves and does not crash, the long press wiring is correct) and
raised three should-fix items, all fixed and re-checked:

1. The floating card revealed the last four with neither device auth nor
   FLAG_SECURE, unlike every other last four reveal in the app. FIXED: it now
   mirrors the flip card exactly, asking for device auth when the phone can lock
   and holding `SecureWindow` while the digits are up (released once on hide,
   timeout, backgrounding, or teardown). Locked by a new test where a refused
   auth stays masked.
2. The skin studio was a bare `Column` that could overflow at a large system
   font. FIXED: wrapped in a `SingleChildScrollView`, like the action sheet.
3. The action sheet preview showed the brand colour, not the card's chosen skin.
   FIXED: seeded from `CardSkinStore`.

Minor items: the reveal haptic no longer fires on a no-op tap;
`_openAccountActions` is debt only today (documented); a deleted card leaves an
orphaned local skin key (cosmetic, ids are never reused).

## Risks and limits

- The floating card's tap to unmask now follows the SAME protection as the flip
  card and the detail screen: device auth when the phone can lock, and
  FLAG_SECURE held while the last four are visible. It offers no copy action.
- Skins currently apply to credit cards (the one full card surface on the tab).
  Extending them to bank and savings rows is a possible follow up.
- The specular sheen is decorative and low opacity so it only brightens the
  face; the card's small text AA contract is unchanged (`bank_card_test`).

## Deferred, named not dropped

- A "Debt avalanche / snowball optimizer" from the earlier brief: a new money
  feature, out of scope for a presentation change, founder gated.
- Applying skins to non credit account rows.
- Carrying a skin choice across a backup restore (would require putting a
  cosmetic key into the backup, deliberately avoided here).

Nothing merged or released by this artifact.

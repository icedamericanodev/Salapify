# f4.25 Band-3 second reminder nudge, implementation report

Founder-approved (2026-08-15) on the behavior-scientist's recommendation, after
the f4.24 fix surfaced that the band cool-off periods (3/7 days) did not drive
the actual 24h reminder.

## Decision

A paused "Big impact" buy (Band 3) now gets a SECOND, gentler safety-net nudge
about 3 days after the pause, in addition to the day-1 nudge. A "Worth a pause"
buy (Band 2) still gets only the one. The full 3/7-day band cool-off is
deliberately NOT used as a single far-out reminder.

Behavior-science rationale (from the behavior-scientist persona): Salapify cannot
put friction on the actual checkout (it cannot block Shopee, GCash, or a BNPL
tap), so a lone far-out reminder only delays our re-engagement and tends to
arrive after an impulsive buyer has already committed, which is the worst outcome
for the riskiest buys. Keeping the decisive nudge at day 1 and adding a day-3
backup for big buys captures the proportional-friction intent without betting
everything on one easily-missed notification.

## What changed

- flutter/lib/money/reminders.dart: in the pure plannedReminders planner, a
  Band-3 waiting item (stored result 'notInPlan') adds a second reminder at
  createdAt + 3 days alongside its day-1 revisit. The add() contract drops any
  entry already in the past, and the whole schedule rebuilds on every app open,
  so a resolved item fires neither. The waiting reminder copy is now
  duration-agnostic ("You paused X. Open Salapify to decide with a clear head.")
  so it stays truthful now that an item can fire at two different times.
- lib/main.dart: stamp f4.25.

## Money and data

READ-ONLY: no money math, no storage change, no schema change. The band is read
from the result key already stored on the waiting item (f-Phase 3).

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues. Full suite green.
- Four new guard tests (reminders_test): Band-3 gets two check-ins (day-1 and
  createdAt + 3 days), Band-2 gets one, the day-3 nudge is dropped once past, and
  the copy no longer hardcodes "24 hours". Existing waiting tests still pass.
- No screen render: this is notification copy and timing, not a screen.

## Deferred (behavior-scientist's further ideas, out of this scope)

- A "+more time" snooze on the reminder / Waiting row, instead of an up-front

Also still open: the three shelf items; a sleeker visual pass; the f4.21 Credit

## Risks

Low. Notification timing only, well-tested, read-only, and the "reschedule on
every open, drop past, surface in Waiting when due" contract is unchanged. Ships
over the air.

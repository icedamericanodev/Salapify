# f4.29 Define the Decision Score, implementation report

Founder feedback (2026-08-15): the Decision Score on Step 2 (Impact) was
confusing and undefined, "I do not understand how to view it". Founder attached a
mockup and asked to make it clearer and define the score in simple words.

## What changed

- flutter/lib/screens/mindset_flow.dart:
  - A "How we score this" link under the verdict opens a plain-words explainer
    sheet that DEFINES the score (a 0 to 100 read of how well a purchase fits
    your money now, higher is safer), lists the three checks it uses (Cash left
    after, Bills and debt, Size vs income), and ends with the honest line "It is
    a guide, not a rule. You always make the final call."
  - The impact card is relabeled to plain words: "WHAT THIS LOOKS AT" with rows
    Cash left after / Size vs income / Bills and debt. "Income unknown" softened
    to "Add income to see".
- lib/main.dart: stamp f4.29.

## Money and data

PRESENTATION AND COPY ONLY. The Decision Score math, the axes, the band
thresholds, and the what-if spectrum are all unchanged. The explainer describes
the existing three-axis engine in plain language; it does not alter it. No
storage or schema change.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues. Full suite green.
- New widget test (mindset_flow_test): the Impact step shows the link, and the
  explainer defines the score in plain words ("quick read, from 0 to 100", the
  three checks, "a guide, not a rule", "Got it").
- Rendered Step 2 and the explainer sheet dark and looked; surfaced to the
  founder.

## Deferred, put to the founder

The mockup's GOAL IMPACT section (a goal picker + Before/After fund bars + "goal
delayed by N days"). The delay/before/after math already exists (goalTradeoff),
but feeding a goal into the flow's decision CHANGES the score (goal axis, weight
15), and the flow deliberately leaves goals out today so the what-if spectrum's
monotonic search stays valid. Wiring it is therefore a money-methodology and
spectrum decision for the founder, not a silent presentation change. Two clean
options to offer: (a) show goal impact as an informational card computed from
goalTradeoff WITHOUT changing the score, or (b) feed the goal into the score
(matching the older mindset.dart screen) and rework the spectrum accordingly.

Also still open: the f4.21 Credit Decision-Score reconciliation; a broader
sleeker pass.

## Risks

Very low. Copy and layout only, no money or data change. Ships over the air.

# f4.53 Accounts tab redesign: expandable category groups

Scope: evolve the Accounts tab from the f4.50/f4.52 chip-filter model into the
founder's new dashboard mockup, an expandable-accordion "Accounts Overview"
with class filters, a hide-balances privacy toggle, and a net worth sparkline.
Built as presentation on top of the same money engine (enhance, never regress).
Founder direction: "Implement this, do not stick to the previous design, lets
continue innovate," with the three-state reference (collapsed, one group open,
all groups open).

## What changed (presentation only)

- **Net Worth hero** gained a kicker row with a hide-balances **eye toggle** and
  a **period selector**, a **trend sparkline** (`NetWorthSparkline` over
  `netWorthWindow`, shown only with two or more points), and its two totals were
  relabelled from "You own / You owe" to **Assets / Liabilities** to match the
  mockup. The owned-versus-owed bar and "View financial position" button stayed.
- **Accounts Overview accordion** replaced the one-tab chip filter. Six category
  groups (Cash & Bank, E-Wallets, Investments, Property, Credit Cards, Loans)
  each collapse to a one-line header (icon disc, name, account count,
  class-coloured total, a chevron that rotates on open) and expand to their
  accounts. A group opens and collapses independently with an `AnimatedSize`
  body that only builds its rows when open. The first non-empty group opens by
  default so the screen never lands all-collapsed.
- **Class filters** (All / Assets / Liabilities / Hidden) with live counts sit
  above the accordion. "Hidden" surfaces accounts left out of net worth
  (archived or `includeInNetWorth: false`), which the counted groups omit, so a
  hidden account always has a home; the chip only appears when something is
  hidden.
- **Real bank logos** show on every account row via the existing
  `InstitutionAvatar`; **credit cards** render as the rich `BankCard` (credit
  variant) inside the Credit Cards group, showing the masked number, network,
  outstanding and limit. A **masked account number** ("•••• 1234") is the row
  sub-line when a last4 is stored.
- **Privacy**: a persisted `settings.accountsHideBalances` toggle masks every
  peso figure on the screen at once (hero, assets, liabilities, month delta,
  available card, category totals, account rows). Credit cards fall back to
  compact masked rows when hidden so no owed figure or limit leaks. The eye,
  the amount, and the semantics all read from one flag.
- The per-category **"+ Add account in X"** button opens the one existing Add
  flow. The old swipeable **carousel** (`_AccountsCarousel`, `_CardItem`) was
  removed; its cards' job moved into the accordion (rows for deposits, rich
  cards for credit), and the flip-card/QR detail is still one tap away on the
  account detail screen.

## What did NOT change (the load-bearing claim)

No money behaviour. `netWorthParts`, `netWorthTrend`, `_countedAmount`,
`countsInNetWorth`, and the account taxonomy are untouched; every figure the
screen shows still folds the same per-row counting it did before, so a group
total can never diverge from the hero. No sign, currency, precision, rounding,
transaction classification, account relationship, schema version, or stored
data was touched. The full golden suite passes unchanged. Nothing a user had
was removed or walled off: Transfer, Pay, the Available card ("Money you can
reach now"), account editing, account detail, and the add flow are all still
present and reachable; archived accounts moved from being mixed into the list
to a clearly labelled Hidden filter, one tap away.

## Category grouping

The six display groups map onto the taxonomy without reclassifying anything:
`cash_equivalents` splits at the e-wallet subtype into Cash & Bank and
E-Wallets; `investments` and `property` are their own groups (Property shows
only when owned); `credit` is Credit Cards; `loans` folds in `installments`.
Every row still resolves through `resolveKind`, so a legacy row with no
taxonomy field lands in the right group with nothing written to it.

## Deviations from the mockup (deliberate)

- **Pan** stays in the hero-adjacent Pan Insight card at the bottom rather than
  the app header; the mockup's colourful per-category icons are drawn instead in
  the Salapify accent (assets) and warning red (liabilities), per the icon rule
  that Salapify-authored icons are theme-coloured Material glyphs, not
  multicolour stickers.
- The **period selector** is an honest chart-range picker (6M / 1Y / All over
  `netWorthWindow`), not a "This Month" delta-period control; the delta stays
  monthly ("vs last month") because that is what the golden-locked trend
  computes.
- The **quick-actions row** (Transfer / Add / Pay / More) and the **Available
  card** are kept though the mockup omits them, because Transfer and Pay are
  real capabilities that removing would regress, and the Available card is a
  shipped P0 figure.
- The **"Customize"** link on the mockup's filter row was omitted (no real
  customize feature exists yet; a non-functional affordance would be dishonest).

## Validation

- `flutter analyze`: 0 issues on the pinned 3.44.6 (the CI build SDK).
- Full local suite green (one row, the QA-log record, added below).
- Coupled tests updated HONESTLY to the accordion model, none weakened: the
  chip-tab tests in `accounts_overview_test` and `accounts_own_owe_test` now
  prove expand-reveals / collapse-hides / class-filter-narrows and the
  per-group total; the carousel tests in `cash_card_test` now prove banks show
  as rows in their group with no PageView.
- Real layout defect found and fixed: relabelling "You owe" to "Liabilities"
  (a longer word) ellipsized in the hero line at 1.0x; the label is now a
  Flexible that fits the shipped Jakarta font and ellipsizes only as a last
  resort. A second overflow (the long "Add account in X" button and the hero
  line at a 1.5x system font in the wide test font) was fixed the same way.
- `screen_readability_test` and `palette_contrast_test` green at 1.0x and 1.5x.
- Rendered the full screen dark, default and all-expanded, looked, and sent the
  founder both PNGs (`test/shots/accounts-full-dark.png`,
  `accounts-expanded-dark.png`).

## Known limitations / deferred

- The manage-debts note renders once per liability group (Credit Cards and
  Loans), a small redundancy kept for now so each group is self-contained.
- Account rows still open the edit sheet on tap (not the richer account detail
  screen); routing rows to detail is a good next step but was left out to keep
  the change scoped and the add/adjust/delete flow test stable.
- An interactive per-category reorder / "Customize" and a set-aside toggle for
  the Available figure remain future work.

## Delivery

Dart only; no new assets, no dependency, no native change, so it ships over the
air. Stamp f4.53. Nothing merged or released by this report.

# f4.16 Money Mindset four-step flow, implementation report

Founder direction (2026-08-14), with a supplied mockup: rebuild Money Mindset
from a single scrolling screen into a guided four-step flow, using Flutter's
real capabilities. This is the flagship.

## Scope

Context to Impact to Decision to Reflection, as a paged wizard with a step
indicator and Back/Continue. Replaces the old single-card Impulse check as the
menu destination. Built on the tested Decision Score engine from f4.15.

## What changed

- New flutter/lib/screens/mindset_flow.dart: the wizard scaffold and all four
  steps.
  - Step 1 Context: purchase-type cards, item and amount fields, category chips,
    Pan's tip.
  - Step 2 Impact: the animated Decision Score gauge, a "what it does" breakdown
    (cushion, income share, bills and debt), and the "what if you spend" card
    (comfortable ceiling plus the zoned spectrum slider).
  - Step 3 Decision: the three reflection questions as an accordion, then a
    band-coloured result (from applyReflection) with the breakdown and the
    Remind me / Skip / Buy anyway actions.
  - Step 4 Reflection: an outcome banner, the 30-day snapshot grid, the mindful
    streak with per-week activity dots, today's lesson, and small wins.
- New premium custom-painted widgets (pure Dart, ship over the air): the
  Decision Score gauge (mindset_score_gauge.dart, animated traffic-light dial),
  the step indicator (mindset_step_indicator.dart), and the zoned what-if
  spectrum (mindset_spectrum_bar.dart, hardened and accessible).
- Engine: mindsetComfortRange and mindsetOneTimeBand (binary search for the band
  ceilings; monotone and exact for the no-goal case; the goal axis is gated out
  and its non-monotonicity documented by a test).
- lib/screens/menu.dart: the Money mindset tile now opens MindsetFlowScreen.
- lib/main.dart: stamp f4.16.

## Design consistency

Everything uses the app's semantic tokens: Radii.card / field / control, the Gap
spacing scale, one 44dp icon rail for the selection cards and a 32dp icon for
input fields so the two input rows match, and a single type hierarchy. These are
the standard every step follows.

## Money and data

READ-ONLY money: the score, the comfort range, and the spectrum only READ the
golden-locked engines (emergencyRunway, safeToSpend, commitmentLoad,
goalTradeoff). No money math, sign, currency, precision, rounding, or schema
changed. The ONLY writes are through the existing, tested store paths, with NO
new schema: addWin on a skip, addMindsetWaitingItem plus Reminders.reschedule on
a remind, and logMindsetCheck on completion. A write failure never traps the
user mid-flow.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues. Full local suite green.
- New mindset_flow_test.dart: skipping records a win and logs the check; remind
  me records a waiting item; the flow never records a transaction (read-only
  money proven).
- Engine vectors (mindset_decision_test): the comfort ceilings are ordered, each
  boundary flips the band, and the band is monotone in the amount; a separate
  test documents the goal-axis non-monotonicity that gates the spectrum.
- Rendered all four steps dark and reviewed each; surfaced to the founder.

## Deferred (a temporary parity gap on a pre-launch beta, flagged)

The original MindsetScreen stays as a component (its menu-nav tests pump it
directly now) until these fold into the flow:
- waiting-queue management (revisit or cancel a paused item);
- manual add-win entry;
- the subscription and credit detail path screens;
- a goal picker with before/after goal bars, which also needs the goal-axis
  monotonicity fix (a money-methodology change) before the spectrum can cover
  goal-linked buys.

## Risks

Low to moderate. The change is large (new primary screen, menu swap), but money
is read-only, writes reuse tested paths, and the write-through and read-only
guarantees are covered by tests. The deferred parity items are the main open
follow-ups.

# f4.66 Your debts in one place (+ PDF), implementation report

Stamp: f4.66 (ships over the air, no version bump)
Branch: claude/salapify-accounts-tab-overhaul-vm3gm6
Source: a founder-provided mockup of a "consolidated liability & billing
statement". Built as the honest version.

## Scope

A "Your debts in one place" screen, reached from the Debts screen, that gathers
every debt the user entered (cards, BNPL, loans) into one overview and a PDF
(Share / Save). It is NOT a bank statement and says so.

## Honesty first: what was removed from the mockup

The mockup carried fabricated trust badges. All were removed before any code:
"Verified Aggregate Statement", "256-bit Encrypted Ledger", "Multi-Banking
Consolidation & Credit Health Report", "Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)
Registered Aggregator", and an "AI" mascot label. Salapify cannot make any of
those claims.

## The money

`consolidatedDebtStatement` (internal name) composes only golden-locked engines:
sum of remaining, sum of capped minimums, sum of `monthlyInterest`,
`creditUtilization` overall, `bankDueDate` per debt, and `debtFreeProjection`
for the minimum-payment warning. It invents no number, so it can never disagree
with the Debts screen.

## Reviews

Two reviews ran before the presentation layer was finalized.

Legal-compliance counsel: engine CLEARED, presentation CHANGES-NEEDED. All
must-fixes implemented:
1. No regulated or lending words anywhere a user sees (no "consolidated",
   "aggregator", "APR", "EIR", "p.a.", "statement of account", "verified", or
   any bank/BSP/SEC name); title is "Your debts in one place"; the stamp was
   reworded to drop "consolidated".
2. "Not a bank statement" disclaimer at the top of the screen and in the footer
   of EVERY PDF page, naming what it excludes (late fees, penalties, annual
   fees, past-due, over-limit).
3. The annualized rate is relabeled from "APR" to "your yearly rate (monthly x
   12)" with the compounded figure beside it, never called the bank's rate.
4. A debt name typed as a card number is masked to "ending 1234" at render time
   (stored name untouched), proven by a test that scans the PDF bytes.
5. A share-time privacy dialog before any export.

Bank-officer: math PASS, wording CHANGES-NEEDED. All must-fixes implemented:
the rate says "nominal" and shows the compounded figure; the minimum-payment
warning was reworded to describe the roll-up `debtFreeProjection` actually
models (not a scarier pure-minimum path it does not compute), with an explicit
never-clears branch; a missing minimum shows as "not set" / a partial total, not
a false zero; and an est-interest caveat says it is today's balances, interest
only, and a full payer owes nothing.

## What did NOT change

No money engine, schema, migration, or stored data. The PDF is generated
on-device and shared only by the user's action; no backend, no transmission. No
new bundled asset, so it ships over the air.

## Deferred (put to the founder)

1. The pre-existing per-account PDF in export_files.dart is titled "Salapify
   statement" and saved as `salapify-statement-*.pdf`, with a comment that it
   "reads like a bank statement". Legal flagged it as a lower-grade version of
   the same risk; a follow-up pass should align it to this honest framing.
2. A debt-entry hint ("enter the rate on your remaining balance, not an add-on
   quote") would protect the interest figures upstream.
3. Surfacing past-due, over-limit, and annual-fee amounts would need new debt
   model fields (a larger money-model change).

## Risks

Low-to-medium, and actively managed. The medium risk is that a formal,
PDF-exportable document could be read as a regulated statement; the legal
must-fixes (no regulated words, disclaimer on every page, masked names, plain
styling with no crest or reference-number field) are built specifically to
prevent that.

# f4.20 Mindset Today "Waiting on" section, implementation report

Founder direction (2026-08-14): after f4.19, the founder chose this from a
three-option menu (the other two, an all-time money-avoided total and a rolling
30-day today grid, were left founder-gated and NOT built). This surfaces the
cooling-off queue that the competitor-UX review had flagged as the feature's
return trigger.

## What changed (presentation + read-only)

- A new "Waiting on" section on the Mindset Today dashboard, between the "See my
  30 days" button and "Recent decisions". It lists the person's paused
  "Remind me in N days" purchases (settings.mindsetWaiting), each with its name,
  estimated amount, and a revisit label: "Ready to revisit" once due, otherwise
  "Revisit in Nh". The section is shown only when at least one item is waiting;
  a reviewed, dismissed, or skipped item never appears.

It reuses the existing, tested pure helpers from mindset_waiting.dart
(waitingItems for the filtered, soonest-first list; isDue and revisitLabel for
the status), so the dashboard and the old waiting screen read the queue
identically. Fields are read defensively, matching the f4.19 crash hardening: a
non-string itemName cannot take the screen down.

## Money and data

No money math, sign, currency, precision, rounding, schema, or stored data
changed. Nothing writes: the section only READS settings.mindsetWaiting, so it
moves no balance and creates no row. The amount shown is the person's own Step 1
estimate.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues. Full local suite green.
- New widget case: the section surfaces waiting items with the correct revisit
  labels, hides a reviewed item, and is absent entirely when nothing is waiting.
- Rendered the dashboard dark and light with two waiting items (one ready, one
  cooling off) and looked; surfaced to the founder.

## Deferred (unchanged founder-gated items, plus one new)

- Still founder-gated and not built: an all-time or 30-day money-avoided sum, a
  composite mindful score, and flipping the today grid to a rolling window.
- New deferral: acting on a waiting item from the dashboard (revisit or decide
  in place). The waiting review lifecycle (Cancel/Undo, the "Do you still want
  this?" prompt, clearing the item and re-logging) lives in the old MindsetScreen
  and is guarded by mindset_waiting_screen_test; wiring it into the dashboard is
  a larger change. The scheduled reminder already brings the person back to
  decide, so this section is read-only for now.

## Risks

Very low. One read-only section on an existing screen; no money, schema, asset,
or dependency change; ships over the air. Covered by a widget test and the
readability sweep (the dashboard is already swept).

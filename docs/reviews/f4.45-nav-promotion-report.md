# f4.45 Bottom-nav promotion (Accounts redesign slice)

Stamp: f4.45
Branch: claude/flutter-3-47-install-e0bx9w
PR: #428 (Accounts redesign)

## Scope

Match the mockup's bottom bar exactly. The bar went from six destinations to
four: Home, Activity, Insights, Accounts. Budget and Utang left the bar and
became pushed full screens, reached from Home and the Menu the same way every
other secondary destination is. Accounts was promoted from a pushed screen to a
resident bar tab. This is a navigation and presentation change only.

Founder direction driving it: "Match the mockup exactly" for the nav, and "push
straight" to complete the promotion.

## What changed

- shell.dart: `Destination.bar` is now `[home, history, insights, accounts]`.
  Accounts icon is `wallet`. `_select` PUSHES Budget and Utang (they are enum
  members still, so Home, Menu, Pan, Search and the course deep-links keep
  naming them) instead of switching a tab. `_pushRoute` wraps the pushed screen
  in a bare `Scaffold(body: screen)` because Budget and MoneyScreen render only
  a `SafeArea` (they were tab bodies) and would otherwise have no Material
  ancestor and no ScaffoldMessenger when pushed. `_popTop` is the back handler.
  Accounts, as the resident tab, is handed `onMenu: _openMenu`.
- accounts.dart: gained an optional `onMenu`; when set (resident bar tab) the
  AppBar shows a `MenuAction`, so the Accounts tab is not a Menu dead-end. Null
  on a deep push, where the AppBar's own back arrow is the way out.
- budget.dart, money.dart: gained an optional `onBack` rendered as a leading
  back arrow in their `ScreenHeader`. money.dart's stale header comment (which
  claimed the strategy toggle survives a tab flip via the shell's outer
  IndexedStack) was corrected, and the now-orphaned `activeController` and
  `showSegment` (used only by the old resident-tab shell wiring) were removed.
- menu.dart: Budget and Utang tiles (Budget via `onSwitchTab(Destination.budget)`,
  Utang via `onOpenPayables`, the "I owe" door).
- screen_header.dart: new `onBack` and a `BackAction` (a plain leading arrow,
  the platform convention, 48 square with a 'Back' tooltip for the screen
  reader). The Menu key stays the raised key.
- salapify_icon.dart: `'wallet'` added to the `_filled` map so the Accounts tab
  has a distinct selected glyph.
- expansion_lesson_reader.dart: the "open Budget" Money Course lesson route now
  wraps BudgetScreen in the same Scaffold-plus-Back the shell uses. It was
  pushing a bare BudgetScreen (no background, no visible back), the exact defect
  this slice fixed in the shell and had left unfixed in the parallel push
  (found by the qa-tester pass).
- main.dart: stamp f4.45.

## What did NOT change

Money meaning is untouched. No values, signs, currency, precision, rounding,
account relationships, transaction classification, calculations, reconciliation,
or stored data were changed. Net worth (`netWorthParts`), transfers, debt payoff
and receivables all read the same golden-locked engine functions. The bar going
6 to 4 and Budget/Utang becoming pushed screens moved no number.

No schema version, migration, backup format, manifest, asset, permission, or
dependency changed. Ships over the air.

No feature was removed or walled off. Budget and Utang are one tap from Home and
from the Menu; Accounts gained a bar seat plus a Menu action; every write path
(Log via the shell FAB on the four bar tabs, debt payments inside Utang, New
utang, Budget quick-add) stays reachable.

## Deviations from the plan

Accounts, as a promoted bar tab, keeps its own Material AppBar (it did as a
pushed screen) rather than converting to the ScreenHeader the other three bar
tabs use. Converting it is a larger UX change outside this slice; the AppBar now
carries the Menu action so nav integrity holds. The header_action test, which
guards the ScreenHeader raised-key placement, was scoped to the three
ScreenHeader tabs accordingly.

## Deferred (flagged to the founder)

- The Snowball/Avalanche debt-strategy toggle no longer persists across leaving
  and reopening Utang. It is transient widget State (never persisted, no money
  or data effect), and losing it on a full close is the inherent, consistent
  behavior of a pushed screen. Persisting it would be a settings/data change and
  a founder call.
- Remaining mockup-fidelity slices already tracked on #428: the overview grouped
  account summary and the quick-actions sheet.

## Risks

Low. Navigation and presentation only, money engines untouched, full suite and
independent QA green. The main behavior change a user could notice is the
strategy-toggle reset above (minor, flagged).

## Unresolved founder decisions

None blocking. The strategy-toggle persistence question is the one open UX
choice, deferred above.

# P2.3 test cases: money set aside

For hand testing on the Android emulator. 2026-10-04.

**Every case below has also been run automatically**, in
`app/test/widgets/p2_3_hand_cases_test.dart`, which taps through the same real
screens and is numbered to match this document. All ten assertions pass. That
is evidence, not a substitute: a widget test cannot tell you whether the
message bar is readable at arm's length or whether the sheet scrolls
comfortably on your phone, and two real defects in this project reached the
founder with the whole suite green.

Running them also found a mistake in case 1 of this document, which has been
corrected below.

## Read this first, it saves you a wasted round

**No peso figures are written down in this document on purpose.** Last time I
quoted figures that came from a test with a frozen clock, you opened the app on
a real date, every number was different, and you went looking for a bug that
was not there. Your emulator runs on today's real date, so your figures will
not match any example. Every case below therefore tells you to **write your own
number down first and then check which way it moved.**

**"FAILS IF" means the test has failed.** It is not a hint to go and find
something. If the FAILS IF line does not happen, the case PASSED. Last time a
column called "WATCH FOR" read as "go and look for this" and you reported a
pass as a failure.

## Case 0: which ledger are you on

This decides which cases apply to you, so do it first.

**Steps**
1. Open the app.
2. Tap **Accounts** in the bottom bar.
3. Look at the list.

**Tell me which of these you see:**

- **A.** An account called **Maya Savings**, and under it the words
  **Sample · Set aside · Maya**.
- **B.** An account called **Maya Savings**, but with **no** "Set aside" on it.
- **C.** A card at the top headed **"Any of these money set aside?"**
- **D.** Your own accounts, no Maya Savings at all.

**B is the expected answer and is NOT a bug.** Your emulator already has a
saved data file from previous testing, and the new sample data only applies to
a phone that has never run the app. Nothing re-writes a file you already have,
which is deliberate: the app must never reach into your stored accounts and
change what they mean.

So: **A, B, C and D are all passes for case 0.** It just tells us where to go.

- Saw **A** or **B**: do cases 1 to 5.
- Saw **C**: do case 6 first, then 1 to 5.
- Saw **D**: do cases 1 to 5 using any wallet or bank account of yours in place
  of the one I name.

## Case 1: the setting exists, and only where it makes sense

**Steps**
1. **Accounts** tab.
2. Tap any **wallet or bank** account (GCash, Maya, BPI, MariBank, or your
   own). The edit sheet opens.
3. Scroll down past **"What is in it now"**.
4. You should see **"What this money is for"** with two buttons, **Spending**
   and **Set aside**, and a grey line under them about net worth.
5. Close the sheet without saving (the **X** at the top right).
6. Now find a **credit card** account (BPI Rewards Card, or any card). It is
   drawn as a piece of plastic rather than as a row.
7. **Tap the card once. It turns over**, it does not open a sheet. That is
   normal, and it is the step I got wrong in the first version of this
   document.
8. On the back of the card, tap **Edit**.
9. Scroll through the whole sheet.

**FAILS IF** the two buttons are missing on the wallet or bank account in step
4, **or** the two buttons DO appear on the credit card in step 9.

The card must not have them. A credit card is money you owe, so "set aside"
would mean nothing there, and a control that does nothing teaches people the
controls do nothing.

## Case 2: setting money aside, and what the app tells you

This is the main one.

**Before you tap anything, write down two numbers:**

- **(a)** The big figure on the **Home** tab, under SAFE TO SPEND.
- **(b)** The big figure on the **Accounts** tab, under NET WORTH.

**Steps**
1. **Accounts** tab.
2. Tap **MariBank Digital Savings** (or, if you do not have it, any wallet or
   bank account that holds a reasonable amount).
3. Note the balance in **"What is in it now"**. Call this **(c)**.
4. Scroll to **"What this money is for"** and tap **Set aside**.
5. Tap **Save changes**.
6. A dark message bar should appear at the bottom for a few seconds.
7. Go to the **Home** tab and read the SAFE TO SPEND figure.
8. Go back to **Accounts** and read the NET WORTH figure.

**What should be true**

- The message in step 6 names **two** Safe to Spend figures, an old one and a
  new one, and says your net worth has not changed and you can still pay from
  the account.
- Home in step 7 is **LOWER** than **(a)**.
- Net worth in step 8 is **exactly (b)**, unchanged to the centavo.

**FAILS IF** net worth changed at all, **or** Home did not move, **or** the
message never appeared.

**One honest exception on the message.** If your Safe to Spend was already at
zero, or the account you picked holds almost nothing, the figure may not move,
and in that case the app stays silent on purpose rather than announcing a
change that did not happen. If that is what you hit, pick a fuller account and
run the case again. That is not a failure.

## Case 3: it still says so afterwards

A setting you cannot see is the same as no setting.

**Steps**
1. Still on the **Accounts** tab.
2. Find the account you just changed in case 2.
3. Read the small grey line under its name.

**FAILS IF** the words **Set aside** are not in that line.

This matters more than it looks. Without it, the only evidence the app is
holding money back is a gap between two numbers on two different tabs, and that
gap looks exactly like a bug.

## Case 4: the app shows its working

**Steps**
1. **Home** tab.
2. Tap the small **(i)** circle at the top right of the SAFE TO SPEND card.
3. The "Safe to Spend Details" sheet opens.
4. Tap the **Audit & Math** tab.
5. Read **step 1**.

**What should be true**

- Step 1 is headed **"Add up spendable cash"**.
- Its description says **"minus ₱…… you have set aside"**, naming an amount.
- The amount it names should match the balance of the account you set aside in
  case 2, which was **(c)**. If you also saw answer **A** in case 0, it will be
  **(c) plus Maya Savings' balance**, because two accounts are then set aside.
- Under step 1 there is a line saying money set aside is still yours and still
  in your net worth.
- Further down, **step 5** is called **"Hold back a surprise buffer"**.

**FAILS IF** step 1 still reads only "Cash, GCash, Maya, banks and debit" with
no mention of money set aside, **or** step 5 is still called "Emergency
buffer".

Step 5 was renamed because once you have a real emergency fund set aside, the
app was calling two completely different things by the same name on the same
screen.

## Case 5: you can still spend it in an emergency

This is the one I would most want to be sure of. An emergency fund the app
refuses to let you spend from would be worse than the original bug, because
your real balance and the app's balance would drift apart.

**Steps**
1. Tap the orange **+ Log** button, bottom right.
2. Make it an **expense** and type any small amount, say 100.
3. Open the **account** dropdown, the one that says which account it comes out
   of.
4. Look for the account you set aside in case 2.

**FAILS IF** that account is missing from the list.

5. Pick it, fill in whatever else is required, and save the entry.
6. Go to **Accounts** and find that account.

**FAILS IF** the words **Set aside** have disappeared from its grey line.

That second check is the important half. Spending from the account must not
quietly un-protect it. If it did, your Safe to Spend would jump back up with
nothing on any screen to explain why.

## Case 6: the one-time card, only if you saw C in case 0

**Steps**
1. **Accounts** tab. The card headed **"Any of these money set aside?"** is
   near the top.
2. Check that **none** of the tick boxes are already ticked.

**FAILS IF** the app has pre-ticked anything. It must never guess which of your
accounts is savings. "Maya Savings" is the real product name of a wallet
millions of people spend from every day, and GSave sits inside GCash, so a
guess would be wrong in both directions.

3. Write down the **Home** Safe to Spend figure.
4. Tick one account you genuinely keep as savings.
5. Tap **Set aside** at the bottom right of the card.
6. The card should disappear.
7. Check **Home**: the figure should be lower.
8. Leave the Accounts tab, go to another tab, and come back.

**FAILS IF** the card comes back.

It must ask once and never again, whether you answered it or dismissed it with
**Not now**.

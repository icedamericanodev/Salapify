# f4.63 Credit Utilization Radar, implementation report

Stamp: f4.63 (ships over the air, no version bump)
Branch: claude/salapify-accounts-tab-overhaul-vm3gm6
Feature source: v4.58 spec, feature 2 of 4 the founder chose to build.

## Scope

A new card on the Debts screen: the Credit Utilization Radar. For every tracked
credit card it shows how much of the card's limit is in use, and the same ratio
across all cards, measured against the 30 percent healthy line.

    utilization = max(remaining, 0) / creditLimit   (per card, type == credit card)
    overall     = sum(balances) / sum(limits)        (cards with a positive limit)

Bands: healthy <= 30%, watch 30-50%, high 50-90%, maxed >= 90%.

## What changed

- `flutter/lib/money/credit_utilization.dart`: new pure function
  `creditUtilization`, plus `bandFor`, `UtilizationBand`, `CardUtilization`, and
  the `healthyUtilizationLine` constant. Composes the same per-card balance and
  limit reads `cardForecast` uses. No interest math, no payoff math, no stored
  data touched.
- `flutter/lib/widgets/credit_radar_card.dart`: new card. Reuses
  `SalapifyProgressBar` and the Barako/AppText system, draws a 30 percent tick
  on the overall bar, shows every row's peso balance, renders over-limit past
  100 percent, and flags no-limit cards.
- `flutter/lib/screens/debts.dart`: card wired in between the TOTAL DEBT card
  and the PAYOFF PLAN card, shown only when there is at least one credit card to
  measure.
- `flutter/lib/main.dart`: stamp f4.63.
- Tests: `credit_utilization_golden_test.dart`, `credit_radar_card_test.dart`,
  `credit_radar_shot.dart` (founder specimen).

## What did NOT change

No money engine, no interest or payoff math, no stored data shape, no schema,
no migration, no new persisted setting. The radar only ratios two numbers the
user already entered. No new bundled asset, so it ships over the air.

## Reviews

Both PH bank-officer and financial-coach PASSED the 30/50/90 bands as defensible
general credit literacy (not a score formula) and confirmed the math is
crash-safe. The convergent must-fixes were implemented:

1. Framing corrected to HEADROOM, not interest and not a score. Interest tracks
   the peso balance carried, not the ratio, so the card never says "get under
   30% to save interest." The header comment was rewritten to say this.
2. A statement-date caveat sits under any non-healthy state, because the radar
   reads the live balance, not the statement balance: pay in full before the
   statement date and the number resets. This protects the responsible
   full-payer with a low starter limit from being mislabelled.
3. Every row shows its peso balance beside the ratio, so a scary percentage on a
   tiny limit is not read as a large debt.
4. Over-limit reads honestly past 100 percent, never capped.
5. A card with no limit saved is flagged and kept out of the overall
   (limitsUnset), the same blank-is-not-a-zero honesty the Safe-to-Spend buffer
   uses.
6. High and maxed statuses point to the payoff plan already on the Debts screen.

## Risks

Low. The figure is a ratio of two user-entered numbers with guarded division and
honest handling of the edge cases (over-limit, no-limit, zero balance, loans
excluded). The main residual risk is a user reading utilization as a score or as
interest, which the framing and the statement-date caveat are built to prevent.

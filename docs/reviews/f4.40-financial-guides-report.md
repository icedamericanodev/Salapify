# f4.40 Financial Guides, implementation report

Date: 2026-08-16
Stamp: f4.40 (not delivered until docs/delivery-log.md shows a row)
Branch: claude/execute-this-4is90d

## Scope

Founder direction, 2026-08-16, with a reference mockup: add a "Financial
Guides" area under Menu > Learn. Two founder choices were taken up front via a
single AskUserQuestion, since content strategy and scope are material forks:

1. Content source: brand-new short explainer articles (not the existing
   multi-lesson courses).
2. Ambition: the full premium hub, matching and going beyond the mockup.

Everything else was routine engineering under the approved phase.

## What this is

A discovery and reading layer OVER content the app already teaches, not a
competing store of facts. 28 short guides (1 to 4 minute reads) across six
topics, browsable through a hub, each linking to the fuller Money Courses
lesson where one exists.

- Hub (`FinancialGuidesScreen`): a subtitle, a horizontal category chip row
  (All plus the six topics), a filled Pan hero ("Learn. Apply. Grow.") with an
  "Explore Money Courses" button into the existing `LearnScreen`, a Popular
  Guides rail (hand-ordered, numbered), a Browse by Topic grid with counts
  derived live from the catalog, a Continue Learning row driven by real
  per-guide progress, and a Pan tip with an "Ask Pan" button into `PanScreen`.
  Selecting a topic swaps the hub for a flat list of that topic, with a
  cross-fade.
- Reader (`_GuideReader`): hero (icon, min-read, topic tag, title, summary),
  prose sections, a "KEEP THIS" takeaway card, a "Go deeper" link into the
  related course, and a finish affordance. Reaching the end, or tapping Mark as
  read, records the guide as read.
- Search (`GuideSearchScreen`): a dedicated guide search over title, summary,
  and topic, so the AppBar search means "find a guide", never the app-wide
  transaction search.

## Catalog (28 guides)

Money Basics 6, Government 6, Tax 4, Investing 4, Debt and Credit 4, Business 4.
Every fact is distilled from the already-verified Money Courses lessons in
`lib/content/lessons*.dart`; no figure was invented. The load-bearing numbers
(the 90,000 peso 13th-month ceiling, the 250,000 / 3,000,000 / 8 percent tax
thresholds, the quarterly BIR dates, the PDIC 1,000,000 per depositor per bank
limit, the BSP 3 percent per month card cap) are lifted straight from the
source files.

## What did NOT change

- Money. No money math, sign, currency, precision, rounding, account
  relationships, transaction classification, reconciliation, or stored money
  data was touched. The guide screens present content; they do no arithmetic on
  amounts. `minutes`, guide counts, and reading percent are metadata, not pesos.
- Existing behaviour. Nothing was removed or moved behind a wall. Money courses
  and Money mindset stay exactly where they were in the LEARN band; Financial
  guides was added above them. Every existing test stays green.
- Schema version, manifest, assets, dependencies. None changed. Ships over the
  air as a Shorebird patch.

## Data (additive, data-safe, no migration)

A new `settings.guideProgress` map holds a per-guide read fraction (0 to 1),
its own namespace exactly like `settings.lessonsRead`. It is preserved by the
backup's `{...settings}` spread with no `backup.dart` change and no migration,
the same additive pattern the lesson and expansion progress already use. Tests
prove it is fully isolated: progressing a guide leaves `lessonProgress` and
`lessonsRead` untouched, and a lesson write leaves `guideProgress` untouched.
Progress can only climb; junk, NaN, and out-of-range fractions clamp instead of
poisoning the map; an untouched or zero-progress guide is never stored, so
Continue Learning never shows a phantom row.

This is the only founder-gated area the phase touched, and it is surfaced here
and in the PR for the founder's review before merge, per the data rule.

## Deviations from the plan

- The Popular row shipped as an `IntrinsicHeight` horizontally scrolling rail
  rather than a fixed-height carousel, so cards are equal height and no summary
  is ever truncated (the readability sweep forbids ellipsized copy). Same
  premium rail, more robust.
- Search shipped as a dedicated guide search rather than reusing the app-wide
  transaction `SearchScreen`. The UX review flagged this as a real fork; the
  dedicated search is the better fit for what the icon means on this screen and
  is within the approved full-hub scope.

## Deferred (founder-visible)

- Showing official government source URLs inside guides. Kept URL-free for
  launch to avoid the fabricated-URL risk the Money Courses rule guards; each
  guide instead links to its fully-cited course. Adding URLs later triggers the
  independent WebSearch verification rule for every cited government URL.
- Wiring the PDIC deposit-insurance figure to read the versioned
  `deposit_insurance_fact.dart` rather than restating it in prose. It is correct
  today and on the same review cadence as its source lesson.
- Deep links from the government, investing, and business guides. Their source
  lessons live in the expansion catalog, which the core deep-link (`lessonById`)
  cannot resolve yet; those guides offer the general Explore Money Courses link.
- A reading chain or streak, a weekly recap, and a "Guide of the day" surface
  (competitor-benchmarked ideas from the design pass, not built for launch to
  keep the first delivery focused).

## Risks

- Content staleness on the volatile figures (PDIC limit, tax thresholds). These
  match the source lessons today; the source lessons carry the review cadence,
  and the guides restate rather than compute, so a lesson update is the place a
  future change lands. The PDIC and tax guides say "check the current figure"
  in their own copy.
- None to money or existing data: the feature is additive and proven isolated.

## Founder decision

Review and approve the PR. Nothing is merged or released. The additive
`settings.guideProgress` data field is the one founder-gated item; it carries no
migration and is proven isolated from money and lesson data.

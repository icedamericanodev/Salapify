# f4.30 Goal-impact card on the Impact step, implementation report

The last piece of the founder's Impact mockup: what a purchase costs a savings
goal. The founder asked an expert to decide how it should relate to the Decision
Score.

## Expert decision (financial-coach)

Informational-first: the goal must NOT enter the Decision Score. The cushion axis
(weight 40, the largest) already carries the emergency fund, so folding a goal in
would double-count it, and a wants goal is the user's own tradeoff to weigh.
Show it as a separate card whose copy never implies the score moved, reuse the
tested goalTradeoff engine, and show a day count only when that engine returns a
real one.

## What changed

- flutter/lib/screens/mindset_flow.dart: a "WHAT THIS COSTS YOUR GOAL" card on
  Step 2 (below the score), with a goal picker (when more than one open goal),
  a Now bar and an If-you-buy-this bar, and an honest impact line. The delay and
  percent-of-remaining come from goalTradeoff; the delay shows only when real,
  otherwise "slows this goal" with no number. Emergency/essential-named goals get
  stronger caution copy, not a different number.
- lib/main.dart: stamp f4.30.

## Money and data

The Decision Score, its axes, band thresholds, and the what-if spectrum are all
UNCHANGED: the flow still passes no goal to mindsetDecision, so the score stays
goal-blind and the slider stays monotonic. The before/after bars use an honest
opportunity-cost framing (buy this and still want the goal means you need the
amount more, so progress is measured against a bigger total), which lines up with
goalTradeoff's own delay math. No storage or schema change.

## Validation

- New widget tests (mindset_flow_test): the goal card shows Now/If-you-buy bars
  and an honest "later" delay, AND the score breakdown still lists only the three

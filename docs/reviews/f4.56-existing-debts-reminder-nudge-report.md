# f4.56 Existing-debts reminder nudge

Scope: Phase 3 of the founder's payment-reminder request. f4.55 shipped a
holiday-aware due-date preview, an Accounts due line, and a one-time opt-in
that offers the Bills due reminder right after somebody SAVES a debt with a
resolvable schedule. That opt-in only fires at the moment of a save, so a
debt that already existed before f4.55 shipped, or one somebody saved and
then declined the ask on, is never offered a reminder again anywhere. Phase
3 closes that gap with a second, complementary surface.

The founder chose, when asked: "Full due-date reminders push" scoped
specifically to "a dedicated Notifications/security screen callout for
existing debts, so people who already have cards or loans get offered the
reminder too, not just new saves."

## What changed

- **A callout on Notifications and security**, inside the existing reminders
  card, directly above the Bills due row. It reads "N of your debts have a
  due date but no reminder set yet." with a "Turn on Bills due" button.
  Computed live on every build from two conditions: Bills due is off, and at
  least one debt has money still owed and a due date that would still queue
  a reminder. It needs no settings flag of its own, unlike f4.55's one-time
  wizard ask, because it is not an interruption anyone can be nagged by,
  only a fact on a screen someone navigated to themselves, and it
  disappears the instant either condition resolves, whether by turning the
  reminder on or by every such debt being paid off.
- **`debtsWithSchedule`** (new, pure, in `money/commitments.dart`): the
  function the callout's count is built from.
- **`billReminderTimes`** (new, pure, in `money/commitments.dart`): the two
  fire times a due date produces (3 days before at 6pm, the due day itself
  at 9am), extracted from `money/reminders.dart`'s bills block so both the
  real scheduler and the new count read the identical rule rather than two
  copies that could drift apart. `reminders.dart`'s own two `add()` calls
  now source their times from this function; the values themselves are
  unchanged, so nobody who already has Bills due on gets a different
  reminder.
- **A `showBillsNudge` test seam** on `NotificationsSecurityScreen`, null in
  production (where `Reminders.supported` decides), mirroring the pattern
  `onboarding.dart`'s own `showNudge` already established, since the widget
  test platform is never Android or iOS and `Reminders.supported` is always
  false there.
- Tapping "Turn on Bills due" calls the exact same `toggle` closure the
  existing Bills due `Switch` already uses: `Reminders.requestPermission()`,
  the identical denial snackbar, then `store.setNotifPref('bills', true)`
  and `Reminders.reschedule`. One permission story, one code path.

## What was found and fixed before shipping

An independent qa-tester pass specifically hunted for the one thing that
matters most here too: can the count ever promise a reminder the scheduler
would not actually send.

- **A real MUST-FIX.** `debtsWithSchedule`'s first draft counted a debt the
  instant `bankDueDate` returned a date `>= today`, but `reminders.dart`'s
  bills block only queues a fire time that `isAfter(now)`. For a debt due
  TODAY, only two fire times exist (3 days before at 6pm, and the due day
  itself at 9am), and past 9am on the due date both are already in the
  past. Someone who opened Notifications and security any time after 9am on
  a debt's due date would see it counted, tap "Turn on Bills due," and get
  zero notifications scheduled for that debt until its next cycle. Fixed by
  extracting `billReminderTimes` so `debtsWithSchedule` checks the same two
  fire times the scheduler itself checks, and only counts a debt if at
  least one is still in the future. Locked by a new test, proven by
  break-then-prove (reverting the check let the due-today-after-9am case
  count again).
- **A SHOULD-FIX (fixed): no re-entrancy guard.** The button's `onPressed`
  called `toggle('bills', true)` directly with no local busy state, so a
  fast double tap could fire two concurrent permission requests before the
  card had a chance to rebuild and hide itself. Not corrupting on its own
  (`Reminders.reschedule`'s own run-token guard already makes the later
  call win), but cheap and worth closing. Fixed with a small
  `_BillsNudgeButton` StatefulWidget that disables itself for the duration
  of one call and re-enables after, whether the call succeeded or was
  denied.
- **A SHOULD-FIX (fixed): an undersized tap target.** The original button
  used zero padding, a 32dp minimum height, and `shrinkWrap` tap target
  sizing, well under the accessibility guideline and visibly smaller than
  the `Switch` two lines below it for the same action. Fixed: 44dp minimum
  height, real padding, and the default `padded` Material hit-area rather
  than `shrinkWrap`.
- **Deferred, not fixed, and named rather than dropped.** The 60-notification
  cap in `Reminders.reschedule` is shared across every reminder kind
  combined (daily, payday, bills, collect, backup, goals, waiting,
  comeback), not new to this phase, and already applied identically to the
  plain Bills due switch before this callout existed. A person with a very
  large number of qualifying debts could in theory see a count the cap
  cannot fully honor once every other reminder kind's own budget is
  accounted for. Not fixed here: doing so honestly would mean replicating a
  large slice of `plannedReminders`'s own budget accounting inside a
  settings-screen render, for an edge case realistic Salapify debt counts
  sit far under. No thousands separator on a large N was likewise judged
  cosmetic and left alone; the label sits inside an `Expanded` and wraps,
  so there is no overflow risk either way.
- **Confirmed already correct, no change needed.** `debtsWithSchedule`'s
  handling of junk `dueDay` values, a non-`List` `debts` field, and
  non-`Map` entries; the singular/plural grammar at the N=1 boundary; no
  peso figure or debt name anywhere in the callout, matching the privacy
  convention every other due-date surface already follows.

## What did NOT change

`money/phcalendar.dart` and `bankDueDate` are untouched. `reminders.dart`'s
bills block computes byte-for-byte the same two fire times as before;
`billReminderTimes` only gives those two literals one shared name instead of
two copies, so nobody who already has Bills due turned on sees any
different reminder, at any time, for any debt. No schema change: the
callout reads only `remaining` and the existing due-date fields, and writes
nothing of its own, not even a settings flag. `dueDay`, `statementDay`, and
`graceDays` semantics are unchanged. The plain Bills due `Switch` and every
other reminder row on the screen are unchanged.

## Validation

- `flutter analyze`: 0 issues on the pinned 3.44.6.
- Full suite green: 3018 pass, 1 expected gap (`qa_record_test.dart`, no QA
  row for this stamp until this row lands).
- New tests: `debtsWithSchedule` and `billReminderTimes` unit tests in
  `commitments_display_test.dart` (including the due-today-after-9am case,
  proven by break-then-prove), and a new `reminders_nudge_test.dart`
  covering visibility with a qualifying debt, no nudge with no qualifying
  debt, no nudge once Bills due is already on, and no nudge on the real
  default (`Reminders.supported` false, matching the actual platform gate).
- The full existing `reminders_test.dart` suite (every reminder kind) still
  passes unchanged after the `billReminderTimes` extraction, confirming no
  behavior drift in the shared scheduler.
- `screen_readability_test.dart` and `palette_contrast_test.dart` both green
  with the new widget in place.
- Rendered Notifications and security with the lived-in fixture (two real
  debts with due dates, Bills due off), dark, looked at both before and
  after the QA fixes (the taller button still reads cleanly, no overflow),
  and sent the founder both PNGs.

## Deferred / known limitations

- The shared 60-notification cap is not reflected in the callout's count
  for an unrealistically large number of qualifying debts (see above).
- No thousands separator on a large N (cosmetic only).
- No literal tap-through widget test proves the success path end to end,
  since `Reminders.requestPermission` has no injection seam and is gated on
  the real platform check; the disappearance behavior itself is proven by
  the existing store-state test instead.

## Delivery

Dart only; no new asset, no new dependency, no native change. Ships over
the air. Stamp f4.56. Nothing merged or released by this report.

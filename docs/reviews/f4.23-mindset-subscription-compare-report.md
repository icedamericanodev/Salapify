# f4.23 Subscription in-flow comparison, implementation report

A small enhancement to the Subscription path shipped in f4.22. When a person is
considering a subscription in the Money Mindset flow, Step 1 now shows a
Monthly/Yearly toggle and a comparison card.

## What changed

- flutter/lib/screens/mindset_flow.dart: the Subscription Step-1 detail gained a
  "Billed" Monthly/Yearly segmented control and a comparison card that shows the
  entered price as both per-month and per-year, and, when the person already has
  subscriptions, what this one would add to their running monthly total ("Your
  subscriptions would go to X a month").
- lib/main.dart: stamp f4.23.

## Money and data

READ-ONLY and presentation-only. It normalizes the entered price the same way
the f4.22 overview engine does (annual / 12, monthly x 12), so the two figures
always reconcile. No new storage, no schema change, no balance moved, and the
Decision Score engine is untouched.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues. Full suite green.
- New widget test (mindset_flow_test): Per month and Per year both show, 499
  monthly reads as 5,988 a year, and switching to Yearly makes 499 the yearly
  figure at about 41.58 a month.
- Rendered the Subscription Step 1 dark and looked; surfaced to the founder.

## Deferred

The Decision-result copy polish; the three shelf items; a sleeker visual pass;
and the f4.21 Credit Decision-Score reconciliation.

## Risks

Very low. Presentation-only, reuses vector-pinned normalization, writes nothing.
Ships over the air.

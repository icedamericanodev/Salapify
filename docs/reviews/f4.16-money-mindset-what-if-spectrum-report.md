# f4.16 Money Mindset what-if spectrum, implementation report

Builds on f4.15's Decision Score. Turns the one-shot score into an exploration:
a personalized comfortable spending ceiling and a draggable, zoned spectrum bar.
Read-only, additive, one-time flow only.

## Scope

f4.15 answered "what does THIS amount do to my money". f4.16 answers the more
useful question the score makes possible: "how much could I spend right now and
still be comfortable, and where does it tip". It shows the largest amount that
still Fits comfortably, and a bar the user can drag to watch a purchase cross
from comfortable to a pause to a big impact.

## The key idea (why it is cheap and exact)

Every axis of the Decision Score worsens as the amount grows: the cushion after
falls, the income share rises, the reserved dip deepens, the goal delay
lengthens. So the score is monotonic non-increasing in the amount, and the band
is monotonic non-decreasing. That means a binary search finds each band boundary
exactly (mindsetComfortRange), and the slider's live band is just the thumb
compared to the two thresholds, so dragging never re-scores the ledger. A test
asserts the band never falls as the amount rises, the property the search relies
on.

## What changed

- flutter/lib/money/mindset_decision.dart: mindsetOneTimeBand (band for an
  arbitrary one-time amount) and mindsetComfortRange (binary search for the
  comfort and caution ceilings). Both read-only, reusing the existing axes.
- flutter/lib/widgets/mindset_spectrum_bar.dart: a new zoned, draggable bar.
  Zones are accent / warning / warningStrong for the three bands; the thumb is
  coloured by the band under it; bandForAmount reads the thresholds purely.
- flutter/lib/screens/mindset.dart: the comfortable-ceiling line ("Up to PHP X
  still fits comfortably right now", floored to a tidy PHP 50) and the bar with a
  live readout, shown only for a one-time buy with a usable amount. The range is
  memoised by a store-money signature so a drag reuses it; the explored value
  falls back to the entered amount whenever the entered amount changes.
- flutter/lib/main.dart: stamp f4.16.

## What did NOT change

No money math, sign, currency, precision, rounding, transaction classification,
schema, or stored data. The whole feature is READ ONLY. The Decision Score
itself is unchanged; this only searches and presents it. The existing verdict,
wins, and waiting queue are untouched.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues.
- mindset_decision_test.dart: new vectors assert the ceilings are ordered, each
  boundary actually flips the band, and the band is monotonic in amount over a
  wide range (spanning all three bands). Plus the f4.15 axis vectors.
- mindset_spectrum_bar_test.dart: bandForAmount at each threshold, and a tap
  maps its x position to a proportional amount.
- Full local suite green.
- Overflow: the 320dp / 2.0x card guard caught a real 447px overflow in the new
  readout row while it was being built; fixed (label ellipsizes, value scales
  down) and the guard is green.
- Rendered dark at a comfortable and a heavy buy and looked; surfaced to the
  founder.

## Deviations and deferred

- The slider is one-time only. A subscription or credit "ceiling" is a monthly
  figure that reads differently; deferred rather than shoehorned.
- Deferred (unchanged from f4.15): the credit cushion-axis money-meaning
  decision, the mindful streak and reflection history (storage), and a
  reflection dashboard.

## Risks

Low. Read-only, monotonicity verified by test, memoisation keyed to the money
state. Worst case of a stale memo key is a briefly out-of-date ceiling until the
next store change, never a wrong money figure elsewhere.

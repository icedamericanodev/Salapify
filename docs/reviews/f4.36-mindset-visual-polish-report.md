# f4.36 Money Mindset premium visual polish

Stamp: f4.36
Branch: claude/execute-this-4is90d
Type: presentation only, ships over the air (Shorebird patch)

## Scope

A "premium" visual pass over the Money Mindset surfaces, the second half of the
founder's "both" direction (the first half, the bank-officer credit polish, shipped
in f4.35). Guided by an independent flutter-ux-craftsman critique. Its ranked safe
items were applied; its risk-flagged items were deliberately left for the founder or
a later pass (see What did NOT change).

## What changed

Presentation only. Nothing here touches money, data, or a write path.

1. Steadier peso figures (tabular digits). Every money and percentage figure that
   sits in a row or animates now uses tabular figures, so digits line up in a column
   and do not jitter width as they change:
   - the Step-2 metric rows (cash left after, size vs income, bills and debt),
   - the Step-2 goal bar percent and the "of <goal>" line,
   - the flow result rows,
   - the subscriptions overview total.
2. One shared hero card shape. The Step-2 "what this looks at" and "what this costs
   your goal" cards, the Step-4 all-time money-kept card, and the subscriptions
   overview card now share one recipe: Radii.hero corner, Insets.hero padding, and a
   subtle primary-tinted fill (alpha 0.08 fill, 0.25 border). They read as one family
   instead of four slightly different boxes.
3. A tighter -0.5 letter-spacing on the big Decision-Score number in the gauge, the
   way large display numerals read best.
4. Haptics on selection. Haptics.select() now fires on every selection tap in the
   flow: the purchase-type card, the yes/no answer button, and the category chip. The
   mode segmented control already had it, so the whole flow now feels its choices.
   No haptic on any failed, blocked, or navigational tap, per the haptic rule.
5. One motion language, reduce-motion aware. Every implicit animation in the flow now
   runs through Motion.of(context, ...) with the shared Motion.curve: the step
   indicator dots and lines, the type and question tiles, the radios, the expand
   chevron rotation, the answer buttons, the category chips, and the step-to-step page
   turn. This unifies the durations onto the Motion tokens (state / move) AND makes the
   whole flow collapse its animations to zero when the user turns on the OS
   reduce-motion setting, which it did not do before.

## What did NOT change

- No money value, sign, currency, precision, rounding, account relationship,
  transaction classification, calculation, reconciliation, schema, or stored data.
  The Decision Score engine, the comfort range, the what-if spectrum, the credit and
  subscription math are all byte-for-byte the same as f4.35.
- The flow stays read-only money; no write path was touched.
- Deliberately NOT done, founder-gated or out of a presentation pass:
  - BoxShadow / elevation on the cards (a heavier look the founder has not asked for),
  - Haptics.moneyWritten on the real write paths (touches money-write feel, deserves
    its own pass),
  - unifying the Mindset Today header into the accent-hero recipe (a material
    product/UX decision on a shipped surface).

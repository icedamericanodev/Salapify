# f4.49 Bank logos follow-up: four more avatar marks

Scope: the follow-up the f4.48 delivery row explicitly deferred, "institutions
with no clean Brandfetch mark stay on initials (Bank of Commerce, CIMB, UNO,
OwnBank, SSS, COL, RCBC), and a higher-contrast RCBC asset could restore it
later." Four of those seven now carry a real avatar mark, pulled via the
Brandfetch connector.

## What changed

Four institutions gain a `symbolAssetPath` (the round-avatar mark only; their
cards keep their existing text because none of the four has a wide wordmark
asset to sit on the white card chip):

- RCBC: the navy hexagon RCBC lockup (Brandfetch dark-theme logo). This is the
  high-contrast variant f4.48's note asked for; the earlier light-cyan hexagon
  that vanished on the white disc is retired.
- COL Financial (id `copstrade`): the circular COL mark.
- CIMB: the red chevron mark.
- OwnBank: the green ring mark.

New bundled assets: `assets/institutions/{rcbc,copstrade,cimb,ownbank}_symbol.png`.
Each was pulled from the Brandfetch CDN, trimmed to its content (dropping the
semi-transparent white halo that would otherwise shrink the mark), size-capped,
and re-checked BY EYE on the white avatar disc at real render sizes (46 and
80 px) before wiring in.

## Asset selection, and what was rejected

The on-white readability was judged by eye, not by Brandfetch's theme labels,
which proved unreliable again here:

- RCBC's "light" logo was white ink (invisible on white); the dark-theme
  variant is the navy lockup that reads. Chosen.
- CIMB's and OwnBank's "logo" assets are lone brand symbols (a red chevron, a
  green ring), not wordmarks, so they became avatar symbols, not card wordmarks.
- BillEase was dropped: Brandfetch's `billease.ph` record is corrupted, its
  wordmark returns the Lazada logo and its icon an unrelated blue owl.
- Bank of Commerce timed out on Brandfetch (504) on every attempt.
- UNO Digital Bank (PH) and SSS have no clean PH record on Brandfetch (the
  search hits are unrelated companies / the US SSA), so both stay on initials.

## What did NOT change

Presentation plus a bundled asset change only. No money math, sign, currency,
precision, rounding, transaction classification, account relationships, schema
version, or stored data touched: `symbolAssetPath` is a code-side catalog
field, user data still stores only an institution id. The account cards for
these four are byte-for-byte unchanged (they had no logo before and still show
text); only the avatars gain a mark. Nothing was removed or walled off.

## Native / delivery

Image assets cannot ride a Shorebird patch, so this needs a new base APK. The
pubspec version bump for the base APK is a founder-gated delivery step; the
`assets/institutions/` directory is already declared in pubspec.yaml (f4.48),
so the four new files are bundled automatically with no pubspec asset edit.

## Validation

- `flutter analyze`: 0 issues (pinned 3.44.6, the CI build SDK, not the
  session default).
- Full local suite green on the pin.
- New guard: `test/institution_assets_test.dart` asserts every declared
  `localAssetPath`/`symbolAssetPath` in the catalog resolves to a bundled file,
  turning a typo (which today falls back to initials SILENTLY on the phone)
  into a red build. Proven it can fail: `File(<real>).existsSync()` is true and
  `File(<bogus>).existsSync()` is false from the flutter/ test cwd, so a wrong
  path makes the `isTrue` expectation fail. It also carries a
  did-anything-happen floor (`checked > 20`) so it cannot pass vacuously.
- `test/logo_preview.dart` re-rendered; its avatar filter now keys on
  `institutionSymbolAsset` so the symbol-only institutions appear. Rendered
  dark, looked, and sent the founder the PNG.

## Deferred

- Bank of Commerce (Brandfetch 504), UNO Digital Bank, and SSS stay on
  initials: no clean mark is available. If Brandfetch's Bank of Commerce
  endpoint stops timing out, or a clean UNO/SSS mark surfaces, they can be
  added the same way.
- No card wordmark chips were added for these four; none has a wide wordmark
  asset, and a square mark squeezed into the 22 px card chip reads worse than
  the existing clean text.

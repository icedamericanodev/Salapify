# f4.28 Act on a waiting item from the dashboard, implementation report

Shelf item 3 of 3 from the founder's Money Mindset shelf: let the person act on a
parked purchase from the dashboard, not only wait for the reminder.

## What changed

- flutter/lib/screens/mindset_today.dart: the "Waiting on" rows are now tappable
  and open a "Do you still want this?" sheet with three deliberate outcomes, each
  a single store write:
  - "No, I skipped it": status -> skipped AND logs the money kept as a win (the
    same addWin the four-step flow's Skip does).
  - "Yes, I bought it": status -> reviewed, no win.
  - "Not sure, keep waiting 24 hours": pushes revisitAt out a day.
  Each reschedules reminders after the write. The rows gained a forward chevron
  affordance.
- lib/main.dart: stamp f4.28.

## Money and data

READ-ONLY money in the Salapify sense: a skip logs a win (records what was NOT
spent, the same methodology the flow uses) and nothing moves a balance or creates
a transaction. No storage or schema change; the item's status/revisitAt fields
already exist. The three outcomes mirror the older mindset.dart revisit prompt's
semantics using the same patchMindsetWaitingItem + addWin store calls, so behavior
is consistent across both screens.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues. Full suite green.
- New widget test (mindset_today_test): tapping a waiting row then "No, I skipped
  it" sets status skipped, adds exactly one win, and creates no transaction.
- Rendered Mindset Today dark and looked.

## Deferred

A sleeker visual pass; the behavior-scientist's "+more time snooze on the
reminder itself" (this adds keep-waiting on the dashboard, not on the
notification); the f4.21 Credit Decision-Score reconciliation. All three founder

## Risks

Low. Read-only money, single-write outcomes reusing existing store methods and
the flow's own win behavior, no schema change. Ships over the air.

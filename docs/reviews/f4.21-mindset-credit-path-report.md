# f4.21 Money Mindset Credit / BNPL detail path, implementation report

Founder direction (2026-08-15), with mockups for the Subscription, Credit/BNPL,
and Decision-result screens plus "experiment, make the UI sleek, and proceed the
three shelf items." This phase delivers the first of those: the Credit / BNPL
detail path. The rest are sequenced follow-ups (see Deferred).

## Money model, verified before building

The founder chose a FLAT ADD-ON fee model (the fee is a one-time percentage of
the price, not a rate per month). A PH bank officer verified the math and copy
BEFORE it was built and returned two must-fixes, both applied:

1. Label it "One-time fee (% of price)", never "interest", with a clarifier: "A
   single fee on the price, not a rate per month. If your plan charges a rate
   every month, this estimate will be too low." A flat 3% and 3%/month differ by
   the term (a 6x understatement at 6 months, 12x at 12), the classic PH add-on
   trap; the label is the safety mechanism.
2. Surface the real cost per year. The computed figures are fed into the existing
   golden bnplCost engine with the fee carried in the monthly and upfrontFee 0
   (the exact integration the bank officer specified), so netCredit is the full
   price and the effective annual rate is honest. Shown only when the engine's
   rateReliable flag is true.

Also applied: the estimate framing ("not a loan offer, fee only before late fees
or penalties, last payment covers any centavo remainder"), and the fee field is
blank by default rather than prefilled with a small rate that would make credit
feel harmless.

## What changed

- New flutter/lib/money/mindset_credit.dart: bnplFlatPlan (extra = price x fee%,
  total = price + extra, monthly = total / months), a Flutter-only helper pinned
  by vectors. Defensive: zero/negative price, a stray zero term, and a negative
  fee all yield safe zeros, never a divide-by-zero.
- lib/screens/mindset_flow.dart: when the purchase type is Credit or BNPL, Step 1
  gains an installment-term segmented control (3/6/12 mo, reusing the shipped
  Segmented widget), the one-time fee field, and a live cost card (monthly
  payment approx, total paid, extra cost, and real cost per year from bnplCost).
  The amount label follows the type (Purchase price for credit, Monthly price for
  subscription, Estimated amount otherwise).
- lib/main.dart: stamp f4.21.

## Money and data

NEW MONEY, but on a founder-directed and bank-officer-verified basis, and
strictly ADDITIVE and PREVIEW-ONLY: it writes nothing, moves no balance, creates
no transaction, and changes no schema or stored data. The annualized rate reuses
the existing golden engine rather than introducing new rate math.

The Decision Score engine wiring is deliberately UNCHANGED in this phase: for
credit it still treats the monthly load as the full price, the pre-existing
shipped behaviour. Feeding the computed installment monthly into the score
instead is a further money-methodology change and is deferred so it can get its
own careful pass and vectors rather than riding in on a UI phase.

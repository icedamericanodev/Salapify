# f4.19 Mindset Today upgrades, implementation report

Founder direction (2026-08-14): "if you can improve it do it experiment,
maximize our resources," on the freshly shipped Mindset Today dashboard.

## How this was aimed

Two independent adversarial critiques ran first and their findings were
synthesised:
- flutter-ux-craftsman (premium polish): its top item was that the richest
  content in the feature, the 30-day reflection, was trapped at the end of the
  four-step flow; it also asked for count-up motion, tappable decision detail,
  and a history filter. It explicitly warned NOT to invent a new 30-day or
  all-time money-avoided sum, nor a composite mindful score, without founder
  sign-off (both are unblessed money arithmetic).
- riley-competitor-ux (pattern benchmark): converged on the same #1 (surface the
  30-day view and the streak) and proposed a rolling window, an all-time saved
  total, and surfacing the cooling-off queue.

The money-sensitive proposals were REJECTED per the craftsman's own warning (see
Deferred). Everything built below is presentation and read-only.

## What changed (presentation + read-only money)

- New MindsetInsightsScreen ("My 30 days"), reachable in one tap from the
  dashboard's new "See my 30 days" button. It is the reflection that used to be
  reachable only by walking the flow: the 30-day snapshot grid, the mindful
  streak with weekly dots, today's lesson, and small wins. It reads the SAME
  golden mindsetSnapshot the flow's step 4 uses, so the two surfaces can never
  disagree about a peso. Its "Spending avoided" is snapshot.confirmedSpendingAvoided,
  not a new sum.
- The weekly-streak dot logic moved to a shared, tested pure helper
  (mindsetWeekDots) and the flow now delegates to it, so the streak reads
  identically on both surfaces and is pinned by a test rather than duplicated by
  eye.
- Count-up motion on the four today-summary figures (CountUpText, the same
  TweenAnimationBuilder pattern the Decision Score gauge uses; OTA-safe). A
  screen reader hears the final value once, not the digits rolling (Semantics
  value + ExcludeSemantics on the tween).
- Every decision row is now tappable and opens a read-only detail sheet (item,
  estimated amount, outcome, the note, the private verdict in the flow's own
  words, and the full date and time). The tile gained an optional onTap and a
  Semantics button label; with no onTap it stays a plain row.
- The View all history gained a segmented All / Bought / Avoided / Waiting
  filter (reusing the shipped Segmented control, so it inherits the
  Jakarta-aware wrap behaviour segmented_test guards), with a per-segment empty
  state and a never-a-dead-end "Log a Decision" button when nothing is logged.

## Money and data

No money math, sign, currency, precision, rounding, schema, or stored data
changed. No new persisted field: mindsetDecisions is unchanged from f4.18. The
insights 30-day money figure reuses the existing golden mindsetSnapshot, so no
new money arithmetic entered the app and the two "money avoided" surfaces (the
today sum and the 30-day snapshot) keep their existing, distinct, already-shipped
definitions.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues. Full local suite green (2988 tests).
- New pure vectors: filterMindsetByOutcome, and mindsetWeekDots (a check today
  lights the newest week, a win 10 days ago lights week 1, junk dates ignored).
- New widget cases: tapping a decision opens its read-only detail with the note;
  "See my 30 days" opens the insights screen; "View all" filters by outcome; the
  f4.18 journey (log a decision, no balance moves) still passes.
- AppBars kept bare per the theme-owned-chrome guard (design_foundation_test).
- Rendered the dashboard, insights, and filtered history dark and light and
  looked; surfaced to the founder.

## Deferred, founder-gated (NOT built, flagged)

- A rolling-30-day or all-time "money avoided" sum over the decision list, and a
  composite "mindful score": both are new money methodology, would need RN test
  vectors and founder sign-off, and risk two screens disagreeing about money.
- Flipping the today grid to a rolling window: changes the founder's supplied
  "Today's Summary" mockup, a material product decision.
- Surfacing the cooling-off waiting queue on the dashboard, a wish-farm goal
  link, a pre-flow breath beat, and a monthly recap: larger product forks.

## Risks

Low. Presentation and read-only; no money, schema, asset, or dependency change;
ships over the air. The main new surfaces are covered by widget tests and the
readability sweep; the shared streak helper is covered by pure vectors.

# f4.38 Money Mindset cash buffer card

Stamp: f4.38
Branch: claude/execute-this-4is90d
Type: presentation only, ships over the air (Shorebird patch)

## Scope

Founder said "proceed" on the deferred Impact-step items from f4.37. This ships the
mockup's third card, "Cash buffer after purchase", which was the largest remaining
piece of "implement this exact screen".

## What changed

Presentation only. No money math, no data, no schema, no write path.

- New `_bufferCard` on Step 2, placed right under the Decision Score card. It leads
  with the peso cash cushion left after the buy (bufferAfter), a status pill, and a
  one-line read:
  - Empties it (warningStrong): "This would use up your cash cushion."
  - Okay, no expense history (primary): "You would still have cash on hand."
  - Thin, under 1 month runway (warning): "This leaves your cushion thin."
  - Okay, 1 to 3 months (primary): "You would still have a cushion."
  - Comfortable, 3+ months (primary): "You would still have a healthy cushion."
- A used-up cushion shows the base-currency zero with the "Empties it" status,
  never a minus figure. The peso amount uses a scale-down FittedBox so a 7-digit
  figure never overflows next to the pill.
- New `_statusPill` helper (tinted rounded chip) for the status word.

## What did NOT change

- No money value, sign, currency, precision, rounding, account relationship,
  transaction classification, calculation, reconciliation, schema, or stored data.
  bufferAfter and runwayAfter are the same engine outputs the breakdown already
  reads.
- No regression: the "What this looks at" card keeps its three signals, including
  the "Cash left after" row that shows runway in months. The new card leads with the
  peso amount; the row keeps the duration framing, so they are complementary.
- Read-only money; no write path touched.
- Deferred, founder-gated, NOT done: remembering the last-chosen goal in the picker
  (needs a stored preference key, low value); a "Comparing against: <goal>" line for
  the single-goal case (the goal name already shows, judged unnecessary).

# f4.52 Accounts P0 polish

Scope: the founder returned a 15-point design brief for the Accounts screen and
sequenced it P0 / P1 / P2. This delivers the founder-approved P0 slice plus the
one P1 they chose (a smarter Pan insight). Dart-only, so it ships over the air,
no reinstall. Money behaviour, data, and schema are untouched.

## What changed (presentation only)

- Net Worth hero restructured. The old side-by-side "Total assets | Total
  liabilities" squeezed each label into half the card and truncated "TOTAL
  LIABILITIES" to "TOTAL LIABILITI..." on a narrow phone. They now stack as
  full-width lines: a status dot, a plain-words label ("You own" / "You owe")
  on the left, and the peso figure with a chevron on the right. Full width
  means the label has room for the whole word, which fixes the truncation
  structurally rather than by shrinking text.
- Ownership caption reworded to "83% assets · 17% liabilities", the two meaning
  colours (assets accent, liabilities warning) joined by a middot, so meaning
  never rides on colour alone.
- Pan in the hero shrank from 76 to 64 so it reads as a small contextual
  assistant rather than competing with the net worth figure.
- The single mixed carousel split into two sections, per the founder's choice:
  "Your Accounts" (deposit and e-wallet accounts) and "Your Cards" (credit
  cards only). Credit cards are the one thing the data can truthfully call a
  card: a debt whose type is a credit card, carrying a limit and a network.
  There is no debit-card concept in the data, so a deposit account is never
  mislabelled a card. Cash stays folded into "Money you can reach now".
- Quick-action tiles compacted (78 to 62px minimum height) to reclaim vertical
  space, the 44px tap target still cleared by the whole tile.
- The Accounts list gained bottom padding so the shell's floating "Log" button
  never traps the last account row or the manage-debts note under it.
- Pan's insight became data-driven instead of one fixed sentence: a rise is
  celebrated, a dip is stated plainly and pointed at the detail, and a steady
  month names the single biggest balance to clear (read from the same debt rows
  the screen already lists). Still kind, never scolding, all read-only.

## What did NOT change

No money behaviour: no value, sign, currency, precision, rounding, transaction
classification, account relationship, schema version, or stored data. The hero
is pure presentation over `netWorthParts` / `netWorthTrend` / `_countedAmount`,
confirmed by the full golden suite passing unchanged. The owned-versus-owed
percentages are computed exactly as before; only the caption wording changed.

## Validation

- `flutter analyze`: 0 issues on the pinned 3.44.6 (the CI build SDK).
- Full suite green: 3000.
- Rendered the real Accounts screen in BOTH themes at a tall viewport, looked
  (dark first), and confirmed: the stacked own/owe lines with no truncation, the
  split Your Accounts / Your Cards sections, the credit card's utilization and
  available-credit read, and Pan's insight on its up branch. PNGs shared with
  the founder.
- `accounts_overview_test` caption assertions updated honestly to the new
  wording (owned/owed to assets/liabilities); no test weakened.

## Deferred, flagged to the founder (P1 / P2)

Not built here because each is new scope or touches data / money meaning the
model does not yet carry:

- Needs Attention layer, account health states, credit due dates and
  utilization warnings. The data model has no reliable last-activity timestamp
  and only day-of-month due days, not full due dates, so these need a data
  decision first.
- A debit-card concept (nothing in the data distinguishes a debit card from a
  deposit account).
- An expandable "Log" speed-dial. The FAB lives on the app shell and serves
  every screen, so a menu there is a global change, out of this screen's scope.
- The P2 design-system items (Figma variables, component variants, responsive
  and state matrices). Note the Flutter side already has design tokens (Barako
  palette, `Gap`, `Radii`, `AppText`, `IconSizes`) and tabular figures baked
  into the money styles, so this is consolidation, not a from-scratch build.

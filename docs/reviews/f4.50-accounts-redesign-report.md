# f4.50 Accounts tab redesign

Scope: rebuild the Accounts tab toward the founder's new mockup, built in
money-safe slices on top of the f4.44/f4.45 redesign (enhance, never regress).
The founder delegated the two open design/money forks to expert judgment ("use
experts and decide"); a financial-coach ruled the liquid definition and a
flutter-ux-craftsman designed the category tabs.

## What changed (presentation)

- Hero: the net worth figure leads with Pan (the bundled coffee-cup mascot),
  total assets and total liabilities split by a hairline each with a status dot
  and a chevron, the owned/owed bar, and a "View financial position" button.
  The greeting moved into the AppBar.
- Quick actions: the primary "Add Account" wears a filled accent disc.
- Pan's Insight card: one plain sentence from the same golden-locked month
  trend the hero shows; opens the trend.
- "Money you can reach now" (the mockup's Available card): the liquid sum,
  replacing the per-cash tiles. Cash still lives, openable, as a list row.
- Accounts by category: a chip filter (Bank, E-Wallets, Investments, Property
  when owned, Liabilities) replacing the stacked own/owe groups, with a
  class-coloured subtotal per tab, a per-tab Add button, and empty-tab states.

## The money question, and the ruling applied

"Money you can reach now" is the one new figure. Per the financial-coach:
count only genuinely liquid asset accounts (cash on hand, savings, checking,
payroll, digital banks, e-wallets); exclude time deposits, investments,
property and all credit; count an unpriceable foreign row as zero (marked with
a tilde) and drop archived/excluded rows via `countsInNetWorth`; sum at the
real signed value (negatives not floored). It reuses the SAME `_countedAmount`
the hero totals and tab subtotals use, so it can never diverge from them. The
title is deliberately "reach now", not "available to spend", so an earmarked
emergency fund in a savings account is not mislabelled as spending money; the
one sentence under it names exactly what is left out and puts bills and savings
first.

## What did NOT change

No money behaviour: no sign, currency, precision, rounding, transaction
classification, account relationships, schema version, or stored data touched.
Net worth, the totals, the trend and every subtotal still read `netWorthParts`
/ `netWorthTrend` / `_countedAmount`. The owned-versus-owed meaning of the old
stacked headings survives in each tab's class-coloured subtotal (a dot, a
colour and the word TOTAL, never colour alone). Nothing a user had was removed
or walled off: cash, every account and every debt are still shown, openable and
editable.

## Category tab mapping

Chips filter resolved rows, not whole categories (Bank and E-Wallets are one
taxonomy category split at the e-wallet subtype). Bank = cash_equivalents minus
e-wallets (so cash on hand and time deposits sit here); E-Wallets = the e-wallet
subtype; Investments = the investments category; Property = the property
category, shown as a chip only when the person owns property so a house is never
labelled an "investment"; Liabilities = credit + loans + installments. Until the
person taps a chip, the section shows the first NON-EMPTY tab, so an e-wallet-only
user does not open Accounts to a blank Bank tab.

## Validation

- `flutter analyze`: 0 issues on the pinned 3.44.6 (the CI build SDK).
- Full local suite green: 2999.
- `screen_readability_test` and `palette_contrast_test` green: the chip colours
  and the red owed subtotal clear WCAG AA.
- Rendered the hero, Available card, Pan insight, and the Bank and Liabilities
  tabs dark, looked, and sent the founder the PNGs.
- Tests updated to the new cash presentation and tabbed list; a `_reveal` /
  scroll helper reaches rows now that the taller screen pushes them below the
  fold. No test was weakened to pass; the intent changes (cash tiles to the
  Available card, stacked groups to tabs) are reflected honestly.

## Deferred, flagged to the founder

- The mockup's "more powerful accounting" right-rail cards (money-distribution
  donut, account health in/out, goals linked, credit overview) are a later
  phase.
- A per-account "set aside / do not count as spendable" toggle (the
  finance-coach's fast follow) so an earmarked emergency fund can leave the
  Available figure while staying in net worth; it should reuse the existing
  `includeInNetWorth` persistence pattern, and it is a data change, so it is a
  founder call.
- Per-tab pre-selection in the Add flow (the Add button opens the generic
  picker for now).
- Whether to use a literal panda illustration (a new bundled asset, so a base
  APK) instead of the over-the-air Pan coffee-cup mascot.

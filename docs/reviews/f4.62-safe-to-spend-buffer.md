# f4.62 Safe-to-Spend buffer, implementation report

Stamp: f4.62 (ships over the air, no version bump)
Branch: claude/salapify-accounts-tab-overhaul-vm3gm6
Feature source: v4.58 spec, the first of four honest features the founder chose
to build. Founder rule for this one, chosen via AskUserQuestion on 2026-08-22:
"Minimum due only, conservative-safe."

## Scope

A new card on the Accounts screen that answers the one question the net-worth
hero does not: "Can I spend this right now, or is it already promised to a
bill?" The card shows a 14-day safe-to-spend buffer and toggles to net worth
for contrast.

The buffer is:

    buffer = liquid cash you can reach now
             minus (credit-card and loan MINIMUM payments due in the next 14 days
                    plus recurring bills due in the next 14 days)

Liquid is cash, e-wallet and checking in the base currency. Savings and foreign
balances are excluded, the same definition the existing golden-locked
`safeToSpend` protects.

## What changed

- `flutter/lib/money/commitments.dart`: new pure function `safeToSpendBuffer`,
  added directly beside `safeToSpend`. It composes only golden-locked primitives
  (`upcomingDues`, `bankDueDate`, `nextOccurrence`, `liquidKinds`, the
  base-currency filter) and does no new arithmetic beyond a windowed sum and a
  subtraction. Returns liquid, cardDue, billsDue, committed, buffer, dueCount,
  minsUnset, windowDays.
- `flutter/lib/widgets/safe_to_spend_card.dart`: new card. Reuses the shared
  `Segmented` control for the Safe-to-spend / Net-worth toggle and the Barako
  and AppText design system. No hardcoded colors, no new money math.
- `flutter/lib/screens/accounts.dart`: card wired in directly under the existing
  "Money you can reach now" (Available) card, so the screen reads liquid first,
  then what is genuinely free after the fortnight's dues. A persisted lens
  setting (`accountsBufferView`) added through the existing setSetting path.
- `flutter/lib/widgets/salapify_icon.dart`: new `info` glyph for the caveat.
- `flutter/lib/main.dart`: stamp f4.62.
- Tests: `safe_buffer_golden_test.dart`, `safe_to_spend_card_test.dart`,
  `safe_to_spend_shot.dart` (the founder specimen).

## What did NOT change

No money engine, no stored data shape, no schema version, no migration. The
existing `safeToSpend`, `upcomingCommitments` and `upcomingDues` are untouched
and their golden tests still pass byte for byte. No value, sign, currency,
precision, rounding, transaction classification or account relationship is
touched. The one new persisted setting is a NON-money view preference with a
safe default. No new bundled asset, so it ships over the air.

## Reviews

Two independent money reviews ran before the UI was finalized.

Bank-officer (PH lending): PASS on the math. Verified the arithmetic by hand
against the golden fixture and traced the composed primitives. Confirmed
"minimum due only" is the correct, non-misleading amount for a "spend without
missing a bill" buffer (subtracting the full balance would be wrong; nobody is
required to clear a revolving card in full). Flagged a real defect: a debt with
NO minimum saved falls back in `upcomingDues` to its full remaining balance,
which for a mortgage would drop a six-figure number into the fortnight. FIXED:
such a debt is excluded from committed and surfaced via minsUnset. Noted three
data-model limitations to defer (dynamic true minimum with over-limit, a
past-due field, a stale flat minimum), all listed below.

Financial-coach: ship it, math is trustworthy. Provided the three-state copy
used verbatim in spirit (healthy names the free figure AND the reserved amount;
thin and negative lead with the constraint, never a spend figure; savings named
as a protected safety net). Required two things the card now does: always label
the reserved amount as MINIMUMS, and never celebrate a large buffer. Confirmed
14 days is a defensible default for the semi-monthly PH pay cadence. Named PH
culture nudges to defer (padala, paluwagan, informal utang, the December
13th-month balloon) and confirmed BNPL is already captured because it lives in
the debts array with a monthly dueDay.

## Deviations from the plan

The proposed "thin buffer" copy state was deferred rather than shipped, because
defining "thin" needs a money threshold and inventing one is founder-gated
methodology. The two shipped positive/negative/no-dues states are honest without
it.

## Deferred (founder decisions, none blocking)

1. Dynamic true minimum for revolving cards (over-limit excess, greater-of
   floor-or-percent). Needs a richer card model.
2. A past-due field so a borrower already behind sees arrears reserved.
3. A December 13th-month override so a cash balloon is not shown as a fat green
   buffer.
4. A "a bill lands just after this window" heads-up for day 15-16 obligations.
5. Nudges to log padala, paluwagan and informal utang so they are reserved.
6. A cross-link from the minimum-only caveat to the coming Avalanche vs Snowball
   payoff tool (feature 3 of 4).

## Risks

Low. The figure is a composition of already-trusted engine values; the main
residual risk is a user reading a delinquency-safe cushion as free money, which
the labeling, the caveat, and the copy are built to prevent.

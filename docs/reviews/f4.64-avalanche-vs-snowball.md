# f4.64 Avalanche vs Snowball comparison, implementation report

Stamp: f4.64 (ships over the air, no version bump)
Branch: claude/salapify-accounts-tab-overhaul-vm3gm6
Feature source: v4.58 spec, feature 3 of 4 the founder chose to build.

## Scope

A new card on the Debts screen, below the payoff plan: a side-by-side of the two
payoff orders, Avalanche (highest rate first) and Snowball (smallest balance
first), showing the total interest and months-to-free of each, with an
extra-monthly selector and an honest verdict.

## The honest heart of it

At the minimums-only, the two orders are IDENTICAL. There is no budget above the
minimums to allocate by priority, so both pay the same total interest and finish
in the same month. The difference only appears when you pay extra. The card
leads with that truth and an extra selector, rather than implying a difference
that is not there at the minimums.

## What changed

- `flutter/lib/money/payoff_compare.dart`: new pure function `avalancheVsSnowball`,
  which runs the golden-locked `debtFreeProjection` once per strategy and
  subtracts. It invents no number the engine did not compute, exactly the way
  `whatIfLadder` composes it.
- `flutter/lib/widgets/payoff_compare_card.dart`: new stateful card with the
  extra selector (Minimums, +1000, +2000, +5000), two strategy columns, and the
  verdict.
- `flutter/lib/screens/debts.dart`: card wired in after the payoff plan, shown
  only when there is more than one debt to reorder.
- `flutter/lib/main.dart`: stamp f4.64.
- Tests: `payoff_compare_golden_test.dart`, `payoff_compare_card_test.dart`,
  `payoff_compare_shot.dart` (founder specimen), plus a `debts` tab render.

## What did NOT change

No money engine change: `debtFreeProjection` is untouched and its goldens still
pass. No stored data, schema, migration, or persisted setting. The extra
selector is ephemeral UI state. No new bundled asset, so it ships over the air.

## Reviews

A financial-coach QA review ran on the copy and verdict for advice soundness
(the strategy math is a composition of the golden-locked engine, and the
strategy definitions are textbook). The verdict was CHANGES-NEEDED, and every
finding was fixed and re-checked:

1. Highest: an asymmetric-null case. When avalanche is payable but snowball
   never catches up (a high-rate debt that is not the smallest balance runs
   away), the verdict used to say "no payoff" while the avalanche column showed
   a real payoff. It now names that as the clearest argument for avalanche, and
   the mirror case too.
2. The single-debt guard also fired at zero debts and told a debt-free user
   they had one debt. Split into a zero-debt and a one-debt line.
3. A Filipino-context caveat added: a debt owed to family or a friend can
   rightly outrank the interest math. Shown only when there is more than one
   debt.
4. A new golden invariant proves avalanche is never worse (interestSaved and
   monthsSaved non-negative) across a spread of books and extras, including a
   0 percent debt, because the copy prints "pays X less" and a negative would
   read as nonsense.
5. The verdict guard now reads `remaining` through `amountOf`, matching the
   golden engine, so a string amount from a backup cannot throw in the widget.
6. The minimums nudge softened from "see avalanche pull ahead" to "see how they
   compare", since equal-rate multi-debt books stay the same with extra.

The verdict is deliberately non-prescriptive: it names avalanche's peso interest
saving AND snowball's behavioral upside (clears a whole debt sooner), and never
tells the user which to pick. It also states, at every amount, that paying the
extra is what actually moves the needle, so the comparison never becomes a
reason to delay.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues on the whole tree, pinned 3.44.6.
- payoff_compare_golden_test.dart: composition properties (avalanche never costs
  more, the saving is exactly the difference of the two engine numbers, single
  debt and equal rates tie, extra passes through), proven by break-then-prove
  (flipping the subtraction turned the +1099 saving into -1099).
- payoff_compare_card_test.dart: same-at-minimums copy, extra surfaces the
  saving, single debt says the order does not matter.
- Full local suite green.
- Rendered dark and looked at, in context and as a specimen sent to the founder.

## Deferred (none blocking)

- A free-entry extra amount (a text field or slider) instead of the fixed chips.
- Showing which specific debt each order clears first, for a fuller motivational
  picture (needs a per-debt payoff-order return from the engine).

## Risks

Low. Both figures are the golden-locked engine's own output at the same extra,
so the card can never disagree with the payoff plan. The main care taken is
honesty: not implying a difference at the minimums, and not steering the user to
one strategy.

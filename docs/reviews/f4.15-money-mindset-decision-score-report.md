# f4.15 Money Mindset Decision Score, implementation report

Phase 3 of the Money Mindset enhancement. Adds an objective 0 to 100 Decision
Score card to the Impulse check flow, built on the app's existing golden locked
money engines. Read only, additive, no regression.

## Scope

The Money Mindset screen already had three purchase types (one time,
subscription, credit), a three way verdict from the reflection questions, a
category budget check, a wins log, and a 24 hour waiting queue. Its only
objective input was the single category budget check. This phase adds the
missing objective picture: a score that weighs a purchase against the person's
real cushion, income, reserved money, and goals, using the same engines Home
and the Afford card already show, so the numbers can never disagree.

## What changed

- New engine, flutter/lib/money/mindset_decision.dart (read only): composes
  emergencyRunway, safeToSpend, commitmentLoad, and goalTradeoff into a 0 to 100
  score across four weighted axes:
  - Cushion after (weight 40): months of expenses the accounts still cover after
    the buy. 3+ months full marks, 1 to 3 ramps 60 to 100, under 1 ramps 0 to
    60, empty is zero. On a thin ledger with no expense history it falls back to
    a 10000 starter floor rather than a fabricated month count.
  - Share of income (weight 25): a one time price against a typical month (5% or
    less full, 50% or more zero); a subscription or credit monthly against the
    NEW total recurring share (35% full, 65% zero, the same worry lines the
    Afford card teaches). Dropped when there is no income history.
  - Bills and debt money (weight 20): whether the immediate outflow dips into
    money reserved before the next sweldo. No dip is full marks; any dip caps the
    axis under 50; a small dip within three days of payday gets sweldo relief.
  - Goal delay (weight 15): how far a linked goal slips. With a deadline, 0 days
    full and 90+ days zero; without one, graded on the share of remaining it
    eats. Dropped when no goal is linked.
  A skipped axis leaves the weighted average, so the score stays 0 to 100 and
  every point is explainable. A credit markup (extraCost / price) costs up to 15
  points. Bands: 70+ Fits comfortably, 45 to 69 Worth a pause, under 45 Big
  impact, impact words never commands (founder decision 2026-08-13).
  applyReflection maps the three private answers to penalties only (never raises
  the score); the "afford without reserved money" No hard caps the band out of
  the green.
- New UI, flutter/lib/screens/mindset.dart: a Money impact card
  (_decisionSection) shown between the consider inputs and the questions the
  moment a usable amount is typed. Band label + score, a one line explainer, and
  the axis rows with a colour dot keyed to each axis score. Barako tokens only,
  AppText typography, label ellipsizes and value scales down so nothing
  overflows at 320dp or large text.
- Stamp bumped to f4.15 in lib/main.dart.

## What did NOT change

No money math, sign, currency, precision, rounding, transaction classification,
account relationships, schema version, or stored data. The card is READ ONLY: it
never writes to the store. The existing verdict logic (_computeVerdict), the
budget impact section, the wins log, and the waiting queue are untouched and
their tests stay green. Nothing a user already had moved behind a wall.

## Visual evidence

Rendered dark on a lived in fixture with several completed months of history so
every axis shows real figures (test/mindset_shot.dart, shots/mindset-impact-*):
- A comfortable ₱1,200 buy: Fits comfortably 100, cushion 4.2 months left, 4% of
  a month, no dip.
- A heavy ₱48,000 buy: Big impact 34, cushion drops to 1.6 months, 150% of a
  month, dips ₱28,699 into bills money.
Both surfaced to the founder in chat, dark first.

## Validation

- flutter analyze: 0 issues.
- mindset_decision_test.dart: 26 hand computed vectors pin every axis to the
  three signed off worked examples (82/Band1, 41 to 16/Band3, 89/Band1).
  Break then prove: flattened the buffer ramp, watched "Expected within 0.01 of
  73.333, Actual 60.0" fail, restored.
- Full mindset suite green (screen, purchase, waiting, wins). The one shared test
  helper change: _openDirect now sets a realistic phone height (800x1200, width
  unchanged) because the default 600px test surface is shorter than any real
  phone and could no longer scroll the lowest question into tap reach with the
  taller card. No assertion weakened.
- Deterministic guards green: screen_readability, palette_contrast, segmented,
  update_stamp.

## Deviations and deferred

- The goal axis renders only when a goal is linked in the UI; verified by unit
  vector, not yet in a rendered shot (would need goal selection in the harness).
- Deferred to later phases: persisting a mindful streak and a reflection history
  (storage, data safe), and a reflection dashboard and entry point.

## Risks

Low. Read only and additive; every figure is an existing golden locked engine
read. The main behavioural surface (the verdict, wins, waiting) is unchanged.

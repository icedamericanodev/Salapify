# f4.51 Pan becomes a panda

Scope: replace Pan the code-drawn coffee cup with the founder-approved
hand-drawn soft-3D panda, across the whole app, and add a new `celebrate`
feeling. Presentation only, on top of the not-yet-delivered f4.50 Accounts
redesign that sits on the same branch, so this one base APK ships the redesigned
Accounts screen with the new Pan already on its hero.

## What changed

- Art: six rendered PNGs under `assets/pan/emotions/` (content, worried, sad,
  angry, tired, and the new celebrate), replacing the five previous cup faces.
  The founder supplied the source art. Processing: a connectivity flood-fill
  from the borders removes the cream field and the soft drop-shadow while
  keeping Pan's cream body, which is ringed by a darker ambient-occlusion
  contour the flood cannot cross. Each is normalised onto one 512px canvas at an
  identical scale and baseline, so swapping feelings changes the face and
  nothing else; celebrate shares that baseline with its confetti and raised arms
  spreading wider, so the burst reads as extra energy rather than a bigger Pan.
- Dark-mode legibility (a real defect, found on review): Pan's dark-brown fur
  was almost the exact value of the warm dark hero wash, so his ears, arms and
  legs dissolved into the background and he read as a floating cream face and
  belly. Fixed by baking a soft cream silhouette halo behind Pan. It lifts the
  dark fur off dark surfaces and is cream-on-light, so it is invisible on the
  light theme and on any neutral surface. Verified on the real hero wash, the
  dark card and the light surface before baking.
- Code (`lib/widgets/pan_mascot.dart`): a `celebrate` value added to the
  `PanEmotion` enum; the two exhaustive switches (`_moodForEmotion`,
  `_emotionWord`) extended to cover it; the header and enum docs rewritten to
  describe the panda instead of the cup. `panEmotionAsset` is unchanged
  (`pan-${e.name}.png`), so celebrate resolved to its file for free. The stale
  "ships over the air with no APK" comment on the Accounts hero was corrected.

## The reactive mapping did not change

`emotionForMood` still maps every ambient PanMood to content or worried only.
celebrate is opt-in by hand for a real win the user earned; it is never an
ambient reaction, so `pan_kindness_test` (the guard that Pan never cries,
scowls or wearies at someone already worried about money) holds unchanged.

## What did NOT change

No money behaviour: no value, sign, currency, precision, rounding, transaction
classification, account relationship, schema version, or stored data touched.
The mascot is decoration; every peso figure on every screen still reads
`netWorthParts` / `netWorthTrend` / `_countedAmount`. No migration, no
backup-format change, no security or privacy surface.

## Validation

- `flutter analyze`: 0 issues on the pinned 3.44.6 (the CI build SDK). The new
  enum value forced both exhaustive switches to be handled.
- Full local suite green: 2999 (2998 before the panda plus the new asset guard).
- New guard `test/pan_asset_test.dart`: every `PanEmotion.values` entry has a
  real, non-trivial PNG at its `panEmotionAsset`. Proven failing first by moving
  `pan-celebrate.png` aside and watching it name the missing file, then restored.
- Rendered the real Accounts hero and the emotion set in BOTH themes through the
  actual `PanMascot` widget, looked (dark first), and sent the founder the PNGs.
  The render harness is committed at `test/pan_panda_preview.dart` (no `_test`
  suffix, so `flutter test` never collects it) for the next Pan change.

## Deferred, flagged to the founder

- The recap and milestone share cards, and the rare asset-load-failure
  fallback, still paint the earlier code-drawn cup through `PanCupPainter`.
  Converting them to the panda art is a follow-up because it needs its own
  render of the EXPORTED card and must keep the baked-palette brand contract
  (a share image must never inherit the sender's theme).
- Two extra source images (a landscape hugging pose without the cup, and a tight
  content bust-crop) are good marketing assets but were not added to the in-app
  set, which needs consistent full-body framing.

## Delivery

Shorebird cannot patch assets (`UnpatchableChangeException`; see
docs/lunch-and-learn.md), so a change to Pan's art is a BASE APK the founder
installs by hand, never over the air. `pubspec.yaml` bumped to `0.9.4+19` to
force the new base APK; stamp `f4.51`. This is the loud flag: the founder must
manually install the new APK once it is built, or nothing new reaches the phone.
Nothing merged or released.

# f4.54 Add a debt redesign: the guided wizard, an interest estimate, Pan back

Scope: Phase 1 of the founder's new "Add credit card" vision. Rebuild the debt
form (`showDebtFormSheet` / `DebtFormSheet` in `flutter/lib/screens/debts.dart`)
from one long wall of truncating fields into a short guided wizard with a live
card preview, add a per-month / per-year interest input with a live estimated
monthly cost, and put Pan back in the Accounts header. Founder-directed, with
the two answered decisions: a per-month / per-year toggle for interest, and
shipping the wizard + interest + Pan first with holiday-aware reminders as a
dedicated Phase 2.

## What changed (presentation, plus one guarded money-display addition)

- **A 4-step wizard** (The basics, What you owe, Payment schedule, Review) with
  a drag handle, a back/close control, a tappable step-progress bar, and a
  pinned footer so the primary action never scrolls away. The steps replace the
  old single scroll of ~11 identical dark boxes.
- **A live card preview** at the top of the card flow, code-drawn (no asset,
  ships over the air), filling in the bank logo, name, masked last 4 and network
  as the person types. Non-card debts get a slim summary strip instead, so a
  family loan is not dressed as plastic.
- **Labels above every field** with short in-box hints and wrapping helper text,
  killing the old in-field `labelText` that truncated ("Payment due day of the
  month (optio...", "Interest % per month, on the remainin..."). The awkward
  "0/4" counter on last 4 is gone.
- **A type picker row** (glyph, plain-English label, one-line gloss) opening a
  compact picker, replacing the nine lowercase machine-string chips ("bnpl",
  "credit card") that never used the existing `_typeLabel` map.
- **Interest as a per-month / per-year toggle** with a live **estimated monthly
  cost** callout, an honest caveat, and the load-bearing add-on-rate warning
  promoted to a callout (copy preserved).
- **Pan back in the Accounts header** (`accounts.dart` AppBar), where the
  founder's mockup opens the screen.

## The money handling, verified

The estimate is not new math: it is the SAME golden-locked `monthlyInterest`
(`balance * monthlyRate / 100`, rounded like the RN engine) every other screen
uses, so the figure on the form can never disagree with the rest of the app. A
PH bank-officer review confirmed the arithmetic and the framing, and the split
it recommended is exactly what was built:

- The stored `monthlyRate` is ALWAYS the monthly percent, the single source of
  truth the engine reads. The engine's contract is untouched.
- The per-year toggle is display-and-input only: `_rateAnnual` divides an annual
  entry by twelve to store (`_effectiveMonthlyRate`), and shows a stored monthly
  as `monthly * 12`. Nothing in `debtmath.dart` reads the unit. Default per-month
  for a credit card (how PH card statements quote it), per-year otherwise.
- The estimate caveat names the real gap: "Estimate on today's balance. Your
  bank charges on your average daily balance and may add fees, so the real
  figure can differ." The annual figure is labelled nominal, never effective.

New golden vectors lock the estimate to the centavo in
`test/goldens/monthly_interest_goldens.json` (replayed by
`debtmath_golden_test.dart`): 18% p.a. on 50,000 = ₱750, 0% = ₱0, 3%/month on
12,480 = ₱374, an odd-cents round-down case, and a 20% p.a. → 1.6666.../month on
30,000 = ₱500 case that proves the divide-by-twelve storage leaks no rounding
error. Rendered a real card edit: stored 3%/month shows as 36% per year and the
estimate reads "About ₱374 interest a month" in both units, matching the vector.

## What did NOT change

The save path (`_save`, `saveDebt`, `patchDebtMeta`), every stored field, the
last-4 regex validation, the PIN/CVV safety note, and the debt payment engine
are unchanged. No schema, migration, or stored-data-shape change. The only
money-relevant edit is that `_save` now stores the monthly rate derived from the
unit toggle instead of the raw field, which is the same monthly value for the
default (per-month) case and is covered by the golden vectors.

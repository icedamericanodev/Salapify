# Reminders, phone notifications, the badge, the wipe and Pan

2026-09-19. Branch `claude/flutter-final`, pull request #473, five commits.

## Scope

Four founder requests in one batch, plus two expert reviews commissioned
during it and verified against the code before anything was acted on.

1. Migrate the bell and reminders from the prototype.
2. Migrate Settings completely, and make it ready for a Play submission.
3. Migrate Pan: offline only, knows the user's own figures, knows every
   feature and how an entry flows through the app.
4. Two decisions taken by the founder mid-batch: change the "Offline Only"
   badge rather than remove the currency converter, and build real phone
   notifications now rather than after launch.

## What did NOT change

**No money math moved.** Not one figure, sign, rounding rule or account
relationship. Safe to Spend, net worth, debt payoff, the reconciliation
engine and the tax calculators are untouched. Pan and the reminder engine
both READ the existing engines and neither computes money of its own; the
only arithmetic either adds is calendar arithmetic (days until a due date,
the next instalment date), and that changes no stored value.

**No stored data shape was broken.** `notifications` and `reminderSettings`
join the snapshot under the prototype's own wire names, with unknown
sub-keys preserved through `Extras` exactly as `payday` already was. A
backup written before this reads unchanged; a backup written by the
prototype still opens.

**One deliberate exception, and it is the point of the feature:**
`deleteEverything` destroys data. It is behind two taps, a figure-by-figure
summary of what goes, and an explicit statement that there is no undo.

## Defects the tests found, that were not in the plan

1. **`enablePhoneReminders` threw into the widget** on a device whose
   notification service is unavailable. The guard was only on the save
   path; it moved into `replanNotifications`, where every caller gets it.
2. **"who owes me money" answered with what the user owes.** The keyword
   `owe` matched the wrong branch first. This is the single confusion a
   two-way debt register must never create, and reading the two keyword
   lists side by side did not find it.
3. **An overdue bill would have buzzed the phone every morning for a
   fortnight.** Found by the planner's own test, not by review. Overdue is
   now weekly on a phone and daily on a screen, and the difference is
   pinned.
4. **A journey test asserted against a harness that could not have shown
   the thing.** It pumped `AppShell` bare, where nothing listens for state
   changes.
5. **The empty ledger had never been walked.** Every fixture here is
   deliberately lived-in. Every tab is now walked with nothing in the store.
6. **The filter pills stacked full width.** Found by LOOKING at the render.
   Fourth occurrence of `Container` + `alignment` + no width in this
   repository.

## Deviations from what the experts advised

Both reports were verified against the code before acting. Where they were
right, the claim is now fixed; where a recommendation conflicted with a
founder decision, the founder's decision won and is recorded.

- The legal review recommended changing the badge **and** defaulting the FX
  call off. The founder chose the badge change only. Recorded.
- It recommended not building phone notifications before launch. The
  founder chose to build them. Recorded.
- Its finding that `applyStarterPack` in the prototype **replaces every
  budget limit with no confirmation** was confirmed by reading the source.
  Starter packs are therefore not ported in that shape and are deferred.

## Deferred, with reasons

- **Starter category packs.** The prototype's version silently destroys
  existing limits. A merge-not-replace version is the right build and is
  its own piece of work.
- **The privacy policy document.** The founder chose placeholders for name
  and contact; the text is written but not yet published to a URL.
- **Play launch blockers that are not code:** a real upload keystore (the
  release build is still signed with the debug key), `targetSdk 36`, and
  the store listing. None can be completed from here.
- **The eight remaining `_soon()` controls on Home.**
